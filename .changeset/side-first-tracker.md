Rebuild battle setup and the live tracker around sides, so a 2v1 shows one score, one command point pool and one hand of cards for the allied pair. Deal tactical missions at random when a turn opens, and ask each card its own question at the moment it pays out: what it wants, what meeting it pays, and a way to say it scored nothing. A card that pays at the end of the opponent's turn is settled as the turn comes back, before the next hand is dealt over it, and the list of battles updates itself when someone adds you to one. Missions still in play sit at the top of the hand until they are scored or put back. Battles, shared lists and profiles now live at `/battles/…`, `/rosters/…` and `/players/…` rather than the old one-letter paths, which no longer resolve.
