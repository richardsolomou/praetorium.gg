Apply the enhancements and datasheet rules that were written against a scope the catalogue reads and this app did not: a Master Artisan now adds to its bearer's Wounds as well as to the Toughness of the unit around it, and a Kroot Trail Shaper moves the Kroot it leads. Four such scopes went unresolved, so around a hundred and fifty stat changes across every book quietly did nothing.
