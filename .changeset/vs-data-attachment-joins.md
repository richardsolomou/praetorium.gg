[rosters] Units that join another as part of it, such as Cryptothralls, Canoptek Tomb Crawlers, Servitors, Nork Deddog and Ogryn Bodyguards, need the Leader their rule names, take no Leader or Support place, and allow one of each kind per unit; one with nothing to join no longer makes the list illegal.
