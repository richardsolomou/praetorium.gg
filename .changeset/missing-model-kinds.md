Draw the models a datasheet insists on, so a unit's loadout no longer omits its sergeant. An Eradicator Squad showed two Eradicators and no Eradicator Sergeant, and its wargear was counted two models short; 53 datasheets were missing a model this way, among them Tactical Squad, Kasrkin, Grey Knights Strike Squad and Battle Sisters Squad.
