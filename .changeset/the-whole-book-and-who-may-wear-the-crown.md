Offer the whole book in the picker, and stop hiding what a detachment brings. The picker priced the first sixty datasheets by name and stopped: a Space Marine player scrolled past Inner Circle Companions straight into the vehicles, and Sternguard Veterans existed only if you already knew to search for them. Every faction detachment in the Space Marines book was also missing its points and its stratagems, because the catalogues call that book Space Marines and the rules dataset files it under Adeptus Astartes; it answers to both names now. A detachment that shares one of its six with another — Armoured Speartip's Armour of Contempt and Rapid Embarkation, and a hundred and eighty-four more across the game — no longer comes up short, on the reference page or in the live tracker. Tanks can be armed: the hunter-killer missile, multi-melta and storm bolter beside a Land Raider's fixed guns were never offered, and neither were the Icarus rocket pod, the ironhail heavy stubber or the Rhino's havoc launcher. Tanks can no longer be crowned: nominating a Land Raider or a Repulsor as your Warlord was never legal without the detachment that makes it a character, and a daemon borrowed into a Chaos Space Marine army cannot lead it either. Every box that narrows a list — the picker, the datasheet and faction finders, and the player search — now carries a cross to empty it, and answers to Escape.
