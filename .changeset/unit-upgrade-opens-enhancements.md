A Trygon with its Character upgrade can take Trygon Prime in Subterranean Assault, in the builder and when importing a list. Options that the rest of the unit opens are now offered, such as the Slaanesh weapons of a Daemonic Charioteer with Daemon of Excess, the Desolation Sergeant's vengor launcher and the Hesyr's concussion gauntlet.
