# Catalogue data: Saved lists and interchange

## Saved lists and interchange

Saved rows contain `RosterPick` choices rebuilt against the current catalogue. Writes atomically advance the user-scoped revision; realtime transports carry no private picks. Discovery and profile readers never receive picks, notes, preparation or a variant's base; [Product design](../../product-design.md) owns visibility.

Setup edits use `RosterSetupDialog`'s two-step review; `src/core/rosterSetup.ts` owns the transition. Price the exact candidate through the ordinary construction authority and recheck before confirmation; Cancel and Back save nothing. Account confirmation compares the normalized source inside the durable document transaction so another tab cannot silently rebase the review; saves outside the durable runtime (including the native v3 bridge) use the server expected-version check with one retained operation receipt and need a fresh review after an intervening edit. Guest localStorage is not atomic across tabs. Cover in `e2e/roster-setup-review.spec.ts`.

A pick's custom name (`nickname`, 40 characters) and `note` (500 characters) live in the saved picks, so copies, variants, conversions and frozen copies keep them; Duplicate unit copies neither, and `normalisePicks` omits both so typing never reprices. A nickname is shown above the datasheet name, never instead of it (`nicknamedUnit`), to every reader of the roster and its frozen copies, and travels in readable text and exchange codes. Unit notes are owner-only: `readerRosterFromRow` and battle/league reads strip them for other readers, rendered notes carry `ph-no-capture`, and no telemetry reads them. Legacy `prep.shareNotes` is stripped without changing pending operation inputs or receipts.

A pick's `embarkedIn` lives on the unit a character joined and travels like `attachedTo`, but not in text exports or `normalisePicks`. The builder judges it like setup (`frozenUnits`, `embarkCards`) and clears what stops fitting. Cover in `e2e/roster-embark.spec.ts`.

`saveRoster` and queued `syncRoster` apply `src/core/rosterLock.ts` before the version check, so stale or offline work gets a refusal kept for review. Locking, unlocking and archiving are explicit server operations that need a connection, first sync that roster's queued edits, and advance its version. A lock is not a permission: sharing, export, battles, leagues and deletion are unaffected. Cover in `e2e/roster-lock.spec.ts` and `e2e/roster-archive.spec.ts`.

Variant groups (`baseRosterId`) cannot be changed by saves and outlive their deleted base. A visitor keeps one browser draft, listed on the rosters page; making a second asks them to sign up rather than replacing it. Signing up from that prompt saves the draft to the account under its own ID, so a battle that froze it still links to the saved list; a later-found draft asks before entering an account, and claim retries reuse its ID. Never overwrite an existing visitor draft through an import, a copy or an exchange link.

Imports accept supported text exports (Praetorium, New Recruit, BattleBase, the official app, ListForge's detailed and WTC layouts, Warmind including its ListForge, tournament and official-app texts, and WTC-style text, parsed in `src/core`), not `.ros`/`.rosz`. Verify the New Recruit chapter case against [`rosterText.blackTemplars.fixture.ts`](../../../src/core/rosterText.blackTemplars.fixture.ts), including its allied Assassin and five attachments. Unknown factions refuse; unmatched units or choices are reported for confirmation, never silently dropped or substituted, and every import is reviewed before saving. Name aliases in `src/shared/rosterFiles.ts` match within a datasheet and never choose a differently named one. Imports without explicit model counts use the models their lines name, else the largest stated wargear count, or the unit's minimum size.

`src/shared/rosterFiles.ts` exports only readable Praetorium text through `toGwText`. It retains model counts, equipment, enhancements, Warlord, attachments, setup, detachment points, roster entry numbers and rules-version identity. Exports never carry notes, preparation, waived or optional rules. Cover exports and their import round trip in [`rosterFiles.test.ts`](../../../src/server/rosterFiles.test.ts) and `e2e/interop.spec.ts`.

[`rosterText.fixtures.ts`](../../../src/core/rosterText.fixtures.ts) holds genuine ListForge and Warmind exports, with their provenance; [`rosterTextImports.integration.test.ts`](../../../scripts/rosterTextImports.integration.test.ts) imports them against the snapshot. Replace a fixture only with another genuine export.

`src/core/rosterExchange.ts` owns versioned exchange codes. Run `e2e/roster-exchange.spec.ts` after changing the format or review.

### Saved loadouts

A saved loadout (`unit_loadouts`, [`unitLoadouts.ts`](../../../src/core/unitLoadouts.ts)) is an account's named copy of one configured unit. It differs from a favourite (a datasheet, not a configuration), a variant (a whole roster) and Duplicate unit (within one roster). The picker offers a loadout only to rosters of its army family, in any rules version or detachment. `loadoutPick` moves it into the roster's rules version through `copiedPicks` ([`rosterCopies.ts`](../../../src/core/rosterCopies.ts)), the mapping a conversion uses, and keeps choices the destination lacks so the roster's ordinary pricing reports them for reselection, as it reports unit and enhancement limits. The picker lists each loadout beneath its datasheet ([`pickerRows.ts`](../../../src/client/features/rosters/builder/pickerRows.ts)): searching its name or its datasheet finds it, the filters judge it as its datasheet but with its own price from the roster's pricing, and a loadout whose datasheet the rules version does not offer is listed last with the reason rather than dropped. Its datasheet row states a unit limit before the add, and its menu renames it or deletes it after a confirmation. Renaming changes only the name, under the same validation as saving; names need not be unique. Saving, renaming and deleting need a connection, and saving stops at `UNIT_LOADOUTS_MAX`; adding one is an ordinary roster edit, and deleting one leaves units already added. [Product design](../../product-design.md) owns visibility. Cover in `e2e/unit-loadouts.spec.ts`.
