# Combat simulation

`/simulator` is a public, account-free matchup calculator. Selecting two units automatically calculates shooting and melee as separate attacks against a full defending unit. Each side reuses the catalogue picker, roster pricing, selected datasheet query, and loadout editor. It does not save a roster or change a battle. Weapon alternatives, roster-style weapon profiles, probability distributions, conditions, and both units’ rules stay visible. No phase selection or run button is required.

## Shared interface and context

`CombatMatchup` is the compact, container-responsive interface for both standalone and future embedded use. The standalone page supplies catalogue controls; a roster or battle caller can supply already evaluated `CombatantSnapshot` values and its own unit controls. Each snapshot holds a selected datasheet, model count, and weapon carriers from the same loadout query. The component does not fetch or mutate roster or battle state.

Faction choices include their catalogue icons. Points sit beside the combatant heading; Loadout sits on the left and the roster editor's model stepper sits on the right, following the unit's permitted sizes. Each combatant displays T, Sv, W, InSv, and FNP together in the shared profile grid; the defender's FNP reflects the configured calculation value. The swap button floats over the divider between units. Swapping attacker and defender preserves both units' loadouts and model counts, resets situational adjustments and attack preferences, and recalculates both phases. Swapping is unavailable while either unit is missing, updating, or failed.

The existing `loadoutDatasheetsQuery` accepts detachment IDs, the full roster, and the selected pick index. Its server projection evaluates contextual characteristics and granted weapon abilities, including supported attached-unit effects. The simulator consumes those final values and displays their modifier sources. Future roster and battle callers must preserve that complete context rather than rebuilding an isolated catalogue pick. `CombatContext` accepts separately resolved shooting and melee conditions, Feel No Pain, and source labels; explicit user adjustments override those defaults, and Reset adjustments restores them. Key the matchup by combatant identity when changing units to reset local adjustments.

Same-unit query placeholders preserve controls and the open loadout editor during edits. A pending edit cancels computation and leaves previous estimates visible and muted until current inputs are ready; recalculation and trial counts have no status labels. An error is shown with a retry action. A different unit resets the matchup. Browser tests pause requests to observe these intermediate states, not just their final results.

## Calculation

`src/core/combat.ts` resolves attacks without IO. A browser worker calculates both phases with up to 20,000 seeded Monte Carlo trials each, reducing trials for larger workloads. Identical inputs reproduce identical results. Runtime validation bounds profiles, model counts, dice expressions, and worst-case work per trial. The worker has a 15-second timeout; changing inputs or leaving the view terminates it. Failure in one phase does not suppress a valid result in the other.

`combatLoadout.ts` preserves model ownership before the datasheet combines identical weapon profiles. `combatPlan` defaults to ordinary ranged weapons, or Close-Quarters weapons when those are all the carrier has. The source rules define Pistol as identical to Close-Quarters; both spellings use the same parsing and selection rules. Monsters and Vehicles can use both groups. Melee uses one ordinary weapon per model plus Extra Attacks; a partial specialist allocation uses the shared weapon for remaining models. Defaults follow catalogue order and are not an optimization claim. Alternative weapons and profile modes can be selected above the weapon profiles. Unknown ownership, inconsistent counts, and multiple ambiguous partial melee allocations refuse a combined estimate instead of guessing. A profile with an unsupported ability blocks the phase when selected.

The output counts wounds actually lost, models destroyed, and complete unit destruction. Each attack resolves damage prevention before discarding damage beyond the current model's remaining wounds. Damage does not spill between models, including Devastating Wounds. Targets start at full wounds and must have identical Toughness, Save, Invulnerable Save, and Wounds across their model profiles. Mixed defensive profiles are refused because their allocation groups are not supplied.

The calculation follows the fetched 11th-edition core rules, particularly sections 05 (attack sequence), 13.08 (cover), and 24 (abilities). Cover worsens Ballistic Skill, capped at 6+, rather than modifying the armour save. Hit and wound roll modifiers are capped separately at ±1. Psychic attacks ignore detrimental Ballistic Skill and hit roll modifiers. Automatic hits and wounds are not critical results. With homogeneous target defences and one Feel No Pain value, resolving Devastating Wounds alongside normal attacks from the same weapon has the same outcome distribution as resolving them after its normal damage: successful damage packets use the same damage expression and prevention roll.

Supported weapon abilities are Torrent, Lethal Hits, integer Sustained Hits, Devastating Wounds, Anti, Twin-linked, Ignores Cover, Psychic, Blast, Rapid Fire, Melta, Heavy, and Lance. Target-keyword restrictions are matched explicitly. Duplicate instances requiring a player choice and unknown weapon abilities disable the affected profile. Characteristics accept bounded integers, D3, D6, and sums such as 2D6+3; an unrecognised expression is refused.

## Boundaries

The simulator reads unconditional, numbered Feel No Pain core abilities and uses the best printed roll. Granted Feel No Pain abilities with a source apply automatically only to single-model units, since that projection does not preserve bearer ownership within a squad. An explicit context value or manual adjustment overrides the printed default, including None; Reset adjustments restores it. Conditional rolls and arbitrary army, detachment, datasheet, or defensive prose are not interpreted. Evaluated profile changes are included; further ability effects require supported explicit conditions or remain excluded. Conditions & rules exposes both units' ability text and independent hit and wound modifiers and re-rolls for each phase, cover, and Feel No Pain. Lethal Hits can be declined to attempt a critical wound. Re-rolls only re-roll ones or failures, as selected.

The player decides range, visibility, target eligibility, and whether Heavy or Lance conditions apply. Hazardous damage to the attacker and Precision allocation are excluded. Indirect Fire is unsupported rather than assumed to be direct fire. The standalone picker has no detachment or attached-leader controls; roster and live-battle entry points are future integrations using the same matchup component.

Unit tests cover scripted roll sequences, independent probability checks, model ownership, automatic weapon selection, and evaluated profile effects. Browser coverage exercises anonymous selection, faction icons, automatic shooting and melee, delayed model/loadout edits, swapping distinct and identical units, defensive values and automatic Feel No Pain, responsive layouts, and JavaScript-disabled first frames.
