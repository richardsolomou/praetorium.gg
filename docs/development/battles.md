# Battles

`src/core/battle.ts` owns commands, legality, and the fold; `src/core/battleView.ts` is the only field-visibility decision.

- Submission validates and appends atomically against `expectedSeq`, which covers the whole log. Never automatically resend a `stale` command with a newer sequence; the one exception is the service itself appending a random secondary draw at the current sequence when the commands since left that side's turn and hand unchanged (`sameRandomDraw`), since it picks those cards anyway. Send an automatic write another device may also make against the screen it was worked out from (`onlyAt`), so losing that race answers stale rather than refused.
- A queued offline command keeps its operation ID, sequence, rules revision, time, and random draw through retries: never rebase, revalidate, or redraw it.
- A battle created with only its creator counts its seats per side in `configure-battle`'s `places`; `openPlaces` and `stillFilling` read them. It is a draft (`battle_drafts`) until every place is filled and every army chosen, when `settleDraft` drops the row; the creator removing a player before the start puts it back with `listed`, so the battle counts as created only the first time it is listed. Every list, count and feed skips a draft; `drafts_by_user` is the only read of them. New battle reuses a draft that still holds only its creator and the same opening command, and the server deletes drafts unchanged for 14 days (`DRAFT_RETENTION_MS`).
- Accounts join a draft's open places through its link (`battle_invites`), not by being named at creation; playing both sides fills every open place with an automated account the creator plays (`playBothSides`, kept in `practice_opponents`), and adding a place while doing so fills it too. Those seats show as the army they bring (`playedSeatName`), never as an account: no profile link, no name of their own. A deployment needs one such account per place a battle can hold beyond its creator. Only the creator, the first player on the first side, removes a player, and only from a battle that opened with `places` (one named at creation keeps its players even if a later setup counts places) and has not begun; that retires the link, so they cannot rejoin through it. The fold drops a command naming a player who has left only in a battle with `places`, so no log written before them folds differently.
- Setup's ticks are `set-setup-checked` with the step's index in `SECTION_IDS` (`src/client/features/battle/setup/sections.ts`), so a step is renamed in place and never moved or removed. Index 0 records that a draft's tracking was chosen; a battle without `places` chose it at creation. Adding or removing an ally place is a `configure-battle` with new `places`, and a pair facing one player shares its side's points (`sharedArmyPoints`).
- Every battle with a seated account is public (`mayWatch`). Practice battles stay out of feeds, standings, and records.
- Attaching a roster rechecks ownership, price, and legality on the server, then freezes it; later edits never rewrite a battle.
- Ability alerts belong to the player, not the list (`ability_reminders`, or browser storage for a visitor), keyed by `reminderKey`. Fielding freezes one per holding unit through `fieldingReminders`, so a borrowed list fields the borrower's. Frozen alerts and unit notes are owner-only: strip them from every other read.
- Every seat a player creates belongs to an account; the only guest seats are an imported battle's unlinked opponent and those older battles seated. The table plays every guest seat, a guest's name never enters the log, and the fold, `claimedLog` and the `guest_seats` reads stay so those battles open and replay unchanged.
- Every write in `spacetimedb/src/index.ts` that can change a counted battle's fold (its log, or who holds its seats) goes through `touchBattle`; guest seat writes only touch `touchBattleSeats`, since guest battles never count. The leaderboard and achievements read `battle_results`, which `src/server/standingsRefresh.ts` rewrites only for the battles `battle_changes` names, so a write that skips it never reaches the ratings or medals, and the battle feeds keep ordering it by its stale `battle_activity`.
- `battle_finishes` and `battle_starts` index each battle's fold, filled by `finishedAt` and `hasBegun` and dropped by `touchBattle`, so a list of battles need not replay every log. The log stays the only record: a change to what begins or ends a battle in the fold has to clear these tables.
- Anything outside a battle reads it through its digest (`src/core/battleDigest.ts`), which `battle_feed` returns in place of the log and `battle_digests` stores, keyed by the seats, the log's length and last sequence, and the reading server's release. The key checks itself, so no write has to drop it, but anything new the fold reads must join the key, and a list that needs more of a battle adds it to the digest rather than reading logs: the digest is what keeps a group's or a player's battles from shipping every command.
- `battle_results`, `rating_steps` and `standings` are derived from the logs and only the standings refresh writes them; no battle screen reads them. A refresh runs only once the public `standings` revision moves, so every write that can change a finished battle's result (finishing, reopening or undoing it, claiming or removing its seats, deleting it or its commands) must raise it. Ratings replay from the earliest finish a changed result touches (`ratingsFrom`), so anything that changes which battles count must move it back: `practiceChanged` replays everything.
- A player's medals are rechecked only when `achievement_checks` says a write could have moved them: `touchProduct` on the scopes in `ACHIEVEMENT_SCOPES`, a changed battle result for everyone `battleAchievers` names, or a call to `recheckAchievements`. A write that changes a medal's inputs without one of these leaves stored medals stale.
- Realtime subscriptions carry IDs and counters, never product state; clients refetch through permission-checked reads and fall back to polling when a subscription fails.
- A filtered feed walks stored order in bounded steps (`filteredFeed`), since a match is only known from the digest. `matchesBattle` is the one answer, online and offline; a filtered result is never saved for offline reading.
- A battle's size from its rosters folds two ways (`rosterBattleFormat`): with `sizeFromSides`, each side's lists add up and a duel may play short; without it, as in older logs, a duel's lists must match and a team battle takes its biggest list. Every new `configure-battle` a client or server sends carries `sizeFromSides`, so older logs keep folding to the size they began at; a BattleBase import copies a finished game at its own size and leaves it out.
