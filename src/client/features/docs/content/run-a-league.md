A league is a series of events. In each one everyone seals a roster in secret, the rosters open together at the reveal, and then the players play each other while the results build up. Start one with **New league** on [Leagues](/leagues).

## Set up an event

- **Battle format** is **Duel** (1v1), **Solo vs pair** (2v1) or **Doubles** (2v2).
- **Points** sets the size every roster is built to. In a 2v1 the solo player builds to it and each ally to half. In doubles every player builds to half.
- **House rules** says what lists may do beyond matched play. All off is matched play. Untick a restriction, such as **Points limit** or **Warlord**, to let lists switch it off. Tick **Legends** or **Crucible** to let lists include those datasheets. A list that needs anything you left off can't be sealed. Team events always need a Warlord in every list, because allies choose whose leads.
- **I'm playing too** enters you as a player. Leave it off to run the event without a roster of your own.
- **More options** holds the event's **Details**, a **Player limit**, **Who can find it** (a private link, or listed on the leagues page) and whether **Anyone who joins** gets in or you approve each player.

The format, points and house rules lock once anyone seals a roster. Everything else can change at any time from **Edit league**.

## Fill it

Share the invite link from **Invite players**, or use **Add friends** to enter friends directly. With approval on, answer the requests in the entrant list. **Remove entrant** takes a player out, and their sealed roster with them.

In a 2v1 give each player a solo or allied size and pair the allies. In doubles pair everyone into teams of two. Each team then chooses its Force Disposition and whose Warlord leads before the reveal. Changing a player's size or team clears the roster they sealed.

## Reveal and play

When everyone is in and sealed, **Reveal all rosters** opens every list at once and closes entry. Anyone playing can reveal, not only the organizer, and the reveal can't be undone. Before then nobody sees another list, the organizer included.

After the reveal, players start their league battles from the event page, and finished battles fill the results table. If a revealed roster has a mistake, press the unseal button on that player's roster card, then **Unseal roster**, so they can seal a corrected one. Battles already started keep the roster they began with.

## Run the next event

When this event's games are done, **Start event 2** (and so on) opens a fresh event with the league's settings. Everyone joins and seals again. Earlier events keep their rosters and results, and the event menu at the top switches between them.

The owner can choose **Admins** from their friends in **Edit league**. Admins run the league like the owner but can't delete it. **Delete league** removes every event, entrant and sealed roster. Battles already started stay where they are.
