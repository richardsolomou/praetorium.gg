Press **+** on a unit's row to add it. To read the datasheet first, choose the unit's name, which lists every weapon it can take, then **Add to list**. On smaller screens, open the picker with **Add units**.

The picker shows every datasheet your faction and detachments allow, with allied units in a collapsed section at the end. Each row shows the price and how many you hold, such as **2/3 in roster**. A trophy marks units that at least a quarter of your faction's [top-four tournament lists](/rosters?view=tournament-lists) take. These are lists from tournaments outside Praetorium, as published by 40k Stats and Mini Headquarters. Press the trophy to see how many. A datasheet's page lists the ones that take it.

## Find a unit

**Search units, keywords, abilities…** matches names, keywords and ability names, including keywords your detachments give a unit. Then narrow the list:

- **Points fit** hides anything that won't fit in the points you have left.
- **Unit limit** hides anything you already hold as many of as you may.
- The heart shows only your favourites, which also sort to the top of each group. It needs an account.
- The box shows only datasheets in your [collection](/collection), including those your models can also be. It needs an account.
- The trophy shows only the units it marks.

## Legends and Crucible

Turn on **Legends** or **Crucible** to show those datasheets. The builder doesn't check whether your event allows them, and they have no printed composition or default loadout.
