The picker (**Add units** on smaller screens) lists every datasheet your faction and detachments allow, grouped as Epic heroes, Characters, Battleline, Infantry and so on, with allied units in their own collapsed section at the end. Legends datasheets aren't offered.

- **Search units, keywords, abilities…** matches names, keywords and ability names.
- **Points fit** hides anything that won't fit in the points you have left.
- **Unit limit** hides anything you already hold as many of as you may.
- The heart filter shows only your favourite datasheets, and the box filter only datasheets in your [collection](/collection), including those your models also count as. Both need an account. Favourites also sort to the top of each group.

Each row shows its price and how many you hold, such as **2/3 in roster**. Choose a unit's name to read its datasheet first, then **Add to list**, or press **Add** on the row.
