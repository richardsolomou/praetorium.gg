Games you finished on another battle tracker can join your battles and your record, so your history doesn't start again here. Praetorium can import from BattleBase so far.

## Import your games

1. On **Battles**, choose **Import battles**, then choose the app you played on.
2. Enter your username there and choose **Next**. Praetorium shows a code.
3. In that app, add the code to your profile: on BattleBase, open your profile, choose **Add tagline** or **Edit**, paste the code and save. This shows the account is yours.
4. Back in Praetorium, choose **Import battles**.

This links that account to your Praetorium account, so you can remove the code again. After more games, open **Import battles** and choose **Import battles** again: games already here are left as they are, so nothing comes in twice. To link a different account, choose **Use a different BattleBase account**.

## Your app isn't listed

Choose **Another app** under **Import battles**, name the app, and choose **Send suggestion**. It reaches us as feedback, and any reply arrives in your feedback under the account menu. It helps to say how many games you have there and whether the app can export them.

## What comes across

Each finished, public game becomes a finished battle on the date you played it, with:

- both armies, unit by unit where Praetorium's datasheets match the app's, and as the app listed them otherwise
- the mission pack, missions and deployment
- every secondary card drawn, achieved and discarded, and the points each one scored
- every stratagem used and what it cost
- primary and secondary points for each round, command points as they were gained and spent, and destroyed units
- the turn and phase times, the battle ready bonus, the winner, and any concession

Games from the 10th edition keep their detachments, missions and cards by name. They link to Praetorium's reference pages only where it holds the same thing.

## Your opponent

A game says which account in that app your opponent played from, not who they are on Praetorium. Once they link that account, Praetorium knows, whoever runs the import:

- If your opponent linked their account in that app before you import, they're in their seat from the start.
- If they link it later, they're put in their seat in every game already imported, yours included. They don't need to import anything themselves.
- Until they do, they sit as a guest under the name they use in that app.

Either way, a game comes in once: whichever of you imports first brings it in, and the other joins it.

## Where they count

Imported games are battles like any other: in your record and on your profile, among public battles, and on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard) once your opponent is in the battle too, as with any game against a guest.

## Common problems

### Your BattleBase tagline doesn't show the code yet

Save the tagline on BattleBase, check it shows the whole code, and choose **Import battles** again. The code belongs to your Praetorium account and that BattleBase username, so a code shown to someone else won't work for you.

### Some games were left out

Only finished, public games you played in can be imported. Make a game public in the app, then import again.

### An army shows as a list rather than units

Praetorium rebuilds an army only when it finds every unit in it. Where one isn't in its datasheets, the army shows as the app listed it, with nothing left out.
