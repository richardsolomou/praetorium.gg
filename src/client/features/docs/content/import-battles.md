You can bring finished games from another battle tracker into your record. Praetorium can import from BattleBase so far.

## Import your games

1. On **Battles**, choose **Import battles**, then choose the app you played on.
2. Enter your username there and choose **Next**. Praetorium shows a code.
3. Add the code to your profile in that app. On BattleBase, open your profile, choose **Add tagline** or **Edit**, paste the code and save.
4. Back in Praetorium, choose **Import battles**.

Your account is now linked, so you can remove the code. After more games, do step 4 again. Nothing comes in twice. To link a different account, choose **Use a different BattleBase account**.

Code not found, or games missing? See [Battle problems](/docs/battle-problems#your-battlebase-tagline-doesn-t-show-the-code-yet).

## Your app isn't listed

Choose **Another app** under **Import battles**, name the app, and choose **Send suggestion**. It reaches us as feedback, and any reply arrives in your feedback under the account menu. It helps to say how many games you have there and whether the app can export them.

## What comes across

Each finished, public game becomes a finished battle on the date you played it. It keeps both armies, the mission pack, missions and deployment, secondary cards and their points, stratagems and their costs, round-by-round scores, command points, destroyed units, turn and phase times, the battle ready bonus, the winner and any concession.

Armies come in unit by unit where Praetorium's datasheets match the app's, and as the app listed them otherwise. Games from the 10th edition keep their detachments, missions and cards by name, and link to reference pages only where Praetorium holds the same thing.

## Your opponent

If your opponent linked their account in that app before you import, they're in their seat from the start. If they link it later, they're put in their seat in every game already imported. They don't need to import anything themselves. Until then, they sit as a guest under the name they use in that app.

A game only comes in once. Whichever of you imports first brings it in, and the other joins it.

## Where they count

Imported games count in your record, on your profile and among public battles. They count on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard) once your opponent is in the battle too, as with any game against a guest.
