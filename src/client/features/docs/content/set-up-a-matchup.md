The [simulator](/simulator) calculates exact odds for one unit attacking another. You don't need an account. Signing in adds your saved rosters and recent simulations.

1. Open [Simulator](/simulator).
2. On the **Attacker** side, use **Choose a unit** and search any faction's datasheets.
3. Do the same on the **Defender** side.
4. Set the model count with the stepper beside each unit.

**Try an example** loads an Intercessors against Necron Warriors matchup you can edit. **Swap attacker and defender**, between the two sides, turns the matchup around.

You can also start from any unit's reference page: **Simulate** offers **As attacker** or **As defender**.

## Add a Leader or Support model

Use the **+** beside the side's name. It only lists units that can legally join. The **×** on a row removes that member.

![The attacker card with a Lieutenant attached to an Intercessor Squad.](screenshot:compare-loadouts-add-member 'The + adds a Leader or other member to the attacker')

## Field a unit in its detachments

Use the detachment button beside the unit's loadout and tick one or more. A detachment is greyed out, with the reason, if it costs too much, clashes with one you've chosen, or goes past the most an army can take.

The side gets those detachments' rules, and their stratagems and enhancements join the buffs you can turn on. Choosing a unit from another faction clears them.

## Use a unit from a roster

When you're signed in, choose a roster under **Attacking with** or **Defending with**, then one of its units. The unit picker on the card also starts with **From a roster**.

That side keeps the roster's detachment, enhancements, attachments and army rules. Attachments come from the list itself, so you can't add or remove members there. The **×** on the roster line keeps the unit and its loadout but drops the roster.

As well as **Your rosters**, you can pick from **Friends' rosters** (their public lists), **Lent by friends** and **Friends' sealed rosters**. Sealed lists appear once a tournament or group hidden-list game you can see is revealed, and stay while it's still on.
