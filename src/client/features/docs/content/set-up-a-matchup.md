The [simulator](/simulator) calculates exact odds for one unit attacking another, so you can see what a wargear swap, a Leader or a buff actually changes. It's free and doesn't need an account; signing in adds your saved rosters and a list of recent simulations.

Open [Simulator](/simulator) and stay on the **Units** tab. **Try an example** loads an editable Intercessors against Necron Warriors matchup if you just want to see how it works.

1. On the **Attacker** side, use **Choose a unit** and search any faction's datasheets. Do the same on the **Defender** side.
2. Set the model count with the stepper beside the unit. It stays within the datasheet's legal squad sizes, and the card shows the unit's points.
3. To attach a Leader or Support model, use the **+** beside the side's name. It only lists units that can legally join, grouped as **Squads**, **Leaders** and **Support**. The **×** on a row removes that member.
4. To field a unit in its detachments, use the detachment button beside its loadout and tick one or more. The list shows each detachment's DP and how many are left at Strike Force; one that costs too much, clashes with a chosen one, or goes past the most an army can take is greyed out with the reason. The side then gets those detachments' rules, and their stratagems and enhancements join the buffs you can turn on; **Rules & buffs** names the detachments each side uses. Choosing a unit from another faction clears them.
5. The swap button between the two sides (**Swap attacker and defender**) turns the matchup around without rebuilding it.

You can also start from a datasheet: **Simulate** on any unit's reference page offers **As attacker** or **As defender** and opens the simulator with that side filled in.

## Use a unit from a roster

When you're signed in, the unit picker starts with **From a roster**. Choose a roster from **Your rosters**, **Friends' rosters** (their public lists) or **Friends' sealed rosters** (sealed lists from a revealed tournament or club sealed game you can see that is still on: a club game until it moves to past games, a Swiss tournament until its last round is played), then one of its units. That side then keeps the roster's detachment, enhancements, attachments and army rules, and a line under the unit names the roster. Reopening the picker lists that roster's units; **Rosters** and **All units** go back up. The **×** on the roster line keeps the unit and its loadout but drops the roster.

On a roster side, attachments come from the list itself, so you can't add or remove members there.
