Choose rosters for **Attacking with** and **Defending with** at the top of the [simulator](/simulator), and every unit on one side is matched against every unit on the other. Picking a roster lists its units: choose **The whole roster**, or one unit to compare it alone. The table fills in as soon as both sides are chosen and again after any change; **Cancel** stops it and keeps the finished pairings. Pairings start at full health (or current health in a battle) with default conditions: supported always-on rules apply; cover, range bonuses and conditional buffs stay off.

- **Show** switches between wounds lost, models lost, uncapped kills and destruction chance.
- **Highlight** marks the best target for each attacker, or the best attacker for each target. Each one is judged on its own, not as a plan for the whole turn.
- Click any cell to open that matchup in full.

## Doubles and solo against a pair

A side can hold more than one roster: **Add roster** fights them together, and **×** takes one out. Mix your own lists, friends' lists and sealed lists freely to try a doubles or 2v1 game before it's played.

Opened from a tournament or hidden-list game, each side starts with its players' lists, so a pair fights as one side, and the table starts on **Shooting + melee** with the best attacker for each target highlighted.

**Test targets** in the roster menu (**Roster actions**) opens the same table for the list you're editing.

## Benchmarks

Pick **Benchmark targets** for either side to test against a standard set: Cadian Shock Troops, Necron Warriors, Intercessors, Heavy Intercessors, Terminator Assault Squad, Rhino, Ballistus Dreadnought, Leman Russ Battle Tank and Land Raider. Add up to 50 units from any faction, change model counts, or **Reset benchmarks**. Your set is saved in this browser only.
