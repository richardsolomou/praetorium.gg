Praetorium tells you when something needs you while you're elsewhere: a friend request, a battle someone started with you, or news from a group or tournament you're in. Each notification links to the page it's about.

## Where they show

On a computer, the bell sits just right of your account picture at the bottom of the side menu, with the button that folds the menu at the far right. With the menu folded, the bell sits between your picture and that button. On a phone's browser it's at the top, next to search. A number on the bell counts the notifications you haven't read; choose it to see them, newest first.

In the mobile app, open **More** and choose **Notifications**. A dot on **More** means some are unread. The app can also send them to your phone as push notifications; see [Use the mobile app](/docs/mobile-app#notifications).

## In your browser

Your browser can show notifications while the site is closed, only in a browser where you turn them on and only while you're signed in there. When your browser can show them, Home shows **Get notified about your games**. Choose **Allow notifications**, then allow them when your browser asks. **Not now** hides the offer in that browser.

You can also switch on **Notifications in this browser** under **Preferences** in [Account settings](/profile). The switch applies only to the browser you're using. Switching it off, or signing out, stops them in that browser; after you sign back in, turn them on again.

On iPhone and iPad, Safari shows notifications only after you add praetorium.gg to your Home Screen. The Praetorium app is the simpler choice there.

If the switch can't be changed and says your browser blocks notifications, you turned them down at the browser's prompt. Allow notifications for praetorium.gg in your browser's site settings, then reload the page.

## What you hear about

- **Friends:** someone asks to be your friend, or accepts your request.
- **Battles:** someone starts a battle with you, or a friend invites you to join theirs. An invitation opens the page where you join.
- **Groups:** a player asks to join a group you run, or you're let into one; and a reminder a day before a league round's deadline while your game in it is unfinished.
- **Lists:** an army data update changes the points of a list you saved, if notifications are on in the app on your phone.
- **Tournaments and hidden-list games:** your entry is requested, accepted, added or declined; your sealed list needs sealing again; a reminder to seal before the deadline; the lists are revealed; a round is paired; or the organizer sets or clears your battlefield.

What happens at the table during a game, such as turns and scoring, never sends a notification. Neither does anything you did yourself.

## Read and unread

Choosing a notification opens its page and marks it read. **Mark all read** clears the count at once. Reading one on the web marks it read in the app too, and the other way round.

Doing what a notification asks also marks it read, wherever you do it:

- accepting or declining a friend request;
- joining a battle you were invited to. If the invitation can no longer be used, because the link was stopped or replaced or everyone's army is in, it's marked read too;
- sealing your list, or sealing a replacement, for the event that asked you to;
- accepting or declining a player's entry, if you run the tournament;
- letting a player into your group or turning them away. Once one organizer answers a request, it's marked read for the others too.

A notification says only what you could already see on the page it links to. If that page is gone, such as a deleted battle or tournament, the notification disappears with it.

## How long they're kept

Praetorium keeps your 50 most recent notifications, for up to 90 days, read or not. Deleting your account deletes them.

You can't turn off kinds of notification. Browser notifications are turned off with the switch above, and push notifications in the app in your phone's settings.
