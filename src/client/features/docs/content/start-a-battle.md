A battle is one shared record of the game: setup, phases, victory points, command points, stratagems and casualties. Everyone seated can operate it from their own device, and anyone at the table can answer for either side, so one phone in the middle of the table is enough.

## Start a battle

Open [Battles](/battles) and choose **New battle**, or choose **Start a battle** from a saved roster in the builder to start with that list already on your seat. To play a friend, choose **Battle** next to them on [Friends](/friends): the dialog opens as a duel with them already seated.

1. Pick a **Battle format**: **Duel** (1v1), **Solo vs pair** (2v1) or **Doubles** (2v2). For solo vs pair, also say whether you're solo or on the pair.
2. Fill every seat. Each seat takes one of:
   - **A friend.** They need an account and must already be on your friends list. If you have no friends yet, the dialog offers an invite link and QR code; send it, then pick them once they've accepted.
   - **A guest**, listed under **No account**. Type their name. Guests can only sit across the table from you, never on your side.
   - **A practice opponent**, listed under **Practice opponents**. You run their side with one of your own lists.

   ![The Start a battle dialog with the opponent list open, showing Guest under No account and the practice opponents.](screenshot:track-a-battle-new-battle 'The seat list: guests and practice opponents')

3. Choose **Tracking**: **Guided** or **Manual** (see below). You can still switch during setup.
4. Choose **Start battle**.

![The Start a battle dialog with the Guided and Manual tracking choices.](screenshot:track-a-battle-tracking 'Tracking: Guided or Manual')

If you and your opponent are both entered in the same tournament, Praetorium asks **You are both in a tournament** and offers to play it there, with your sealed lists. Choose the tournament, or **Play it casually** to keep it out of the tournament.

## Guided or manual tracking

**Guided** deals the mission cards and prompts you when they score. You get the caps the mission sets, command points each Command phase, the battle ready bonus, stratagems with their CP costs and limits, and the missed-action tool.

**Manual** is a plain scoresheet. Scores and command points are whatever you enter. There are no mission cards, scoring prompts, caps, automatic CP or battle ready bonus, and no stratagem list.

The choice matters after the game: manual battles don't count on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard). Tournament battles are always guided.
