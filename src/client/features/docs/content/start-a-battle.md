Open [Battles](/battles) and choose **New battle**. Setup opens straight away with just you at the table, as a **Draft**.

![Battle setup on a new draft: your card with Choose army, an empty Opponent card with Share a link, and the Tracking step.](screenshot:track-a-battle-new-battle 'A new battle starts as a draft')

1. Under **Tracking**, choose **Guided** or **Manual**. Choosing your army or sharing the link goes on with Guided.
2. Choose your army on your card at the top. The first list chosen sets the battle size, and a bigger or smaller list changes it.
3. Bring your opponent in under **Opponent** (see below).
4. Answer the rest of setup and choose **Start battle**.

![The Tracking step with the Guided and Manual choices.](screenshot:track-a-battle-tracking 'Tracking: Guided or Manual')

Every step stays on the page. The ones you haven't reached yet are faded until the step before them is done.

A draft stays out of your battles until everyone has joined and chosen an army. **Continue your draft** on [Battles](/battles) takes you back to it, and its **⋮** menu or a right-click deletes it. **New battle** reuses a draft you haven't touched yet. A draft nobody changes for 14 days is deleted.

One phone in the middle of the table is enough, because anyone at the table can answer for either side. Everyone who has joined can also use their own device.

**Start a battle** on a saved roster in the builder or your library opens a draft with that list chosen. **Battle** next to a friend on [Friends](/friends) opens one with them already invited.

## Bring your opponent in

- **Share a link** shows a QR code and a link. Whoever opens it signs in, or makes an account, and joins. **Stop sharing** turns the link off.
- **Invite a friend** sends a friend a notification they join from. If you're both in the same tournament, Praetorium offers to play your game there instead.
- **Play both sides** makes every place yours: you choose every army from your own lists and play them all on this device. Each place is named after the army you choose for it. Until you start the battle, **Invite players instead** gives your places back to the link. Each place you play offers it on its card too. A battle you play alone doesn't count in your record or on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard).

Otherwise everyone in a battle has their own account, so the result goes in every player's record.

Until you start the battle, you can **Remove** a player who joined, in this step or on their card. Their place opens again and their link stops working. A battle with an open place is a draft again until someone fills it and chooses an army.

## Allies

**Add ally** under a side adds a place for another player on that side, so a duel becomes one player against a pair, or doubles. The × next to an empty place takes it away. One link fills every open place, and whoever opens it picks a side when both have room. When you play both sides, the new place is yours straight away: choose its army on the card, or take it away with its ×.

A pair against one player shares its side's points, even before the ally joins. Until the ally chooses a list, they count as bringing one the size of their partner's. In doubles each army brings half its side's points.

## Guided or manual tracking

**Guided** deals the mission cards and prompts you when they score. It applies the mission's caps, command points each Command phase and the battle ready bonus, and gives you stratagems with their CP costs and limits, and the missed-action tool.

**Manual** is a plain scoresheet with none of that. Scores and command points are whatever you enter.

Manual battles don't count on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard). Tournament battles are always guided.
