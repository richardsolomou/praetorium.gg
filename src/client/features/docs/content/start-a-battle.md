Open [Battles](/battles) and choose **New battle**.

1. Pick a **Battle format**: **Duel** (1v1), **Solo vs pair** (2v1) or **Doubles** (2v2). For solo vs pair, also say which side you're on.
2. Fill every seat with a friend, a guest or a practice opponent (see below).

   ![The Start a battle dialog with the opponent list open, showing Guest under No account and the practice opponents.](screenshot:track-a-battle-new-battle 'The seat list: guests and practice opponents')

3. Choose **Tracking**: **Guided** or **Manual**. You can still switch during setup.
4. Choose **Start battle**.

![The Start a battle dialog with the Guided and Manual tracking choices.](screenshot:track-a-battle-tracking 'Tracking: Guided or Manual')

One phone in the middle of the table is enough, because anyone at the table can answer for either side. Everyone seated can also use their own device.

**Start a battle** on a saved roster in the builder puts that list on your seat. **Battle** next to a friend on [Friends](/friends) opens a duel with them already seated.

## Who can sit in a seat

A friend needs an account and must already be on your friends list. If you have no friends yet, the dialog offers an invite link and QR code. Pick them once they've accepted.

A guest is listed under **No account**. Type their name. Guests can only sit across the table from you, never on your side.

A practice opponent is listed under **Practice opponents**. You run their side with one of your own lists or another player's public roster, which you can search by name, army or player under **Public rosters** when you choose their army. A public roster is fielded as it stands and never added to your rosters.

If you're both entered in the same tournament, Praetorium asks **You are both in a tournament**. Choose the tournament to play it there with your sealed lists, or **Play it casually** to keep it out.

## Guided or manual tracking

**Guided** deals the mission cards and prompts you when they score. It applies the mission's caps, command points each Command phase and the battle ready bonus, and gives you stratagems with their CP costs and limits, and the missed-action tool.

**Manual** is a plain scoresheet with none of that. Scores and command points are whatever you enter.

Manual battles don't count on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard). Tournament battles are always guided.
