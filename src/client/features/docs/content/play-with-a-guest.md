You play a guest's seat until they join: you choose its roster from your own lists and record its side. To let them play on their own phone:

1. Choose **Invite to play** beside their name.
2. Show them the QR code, or use **Copy link** or **Share link**.
3. They open the link and choose **Join on this device**. No account is needed. The first device to do this takes the seat for 30 days.
4. They build or import a list in the roster builder, then pick **Bring this roster** in setup.

![The Invite to play dialog with a QR code, the seat link and a Copy link button.](screenshot:track-a-battle-guest-invite "Copy link, under the guest's QR code")

Once they've joined, their prompts and secret cards are theirs alone. A guest who's signed in still plays as the guest seat. Guest seats need an internet connection.

If they lose their phone or switch device, open **Their device** and choose **Take back [name]'s seat**. You play their side again, and a fresh link appears for them.
