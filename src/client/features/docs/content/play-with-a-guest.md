A guest seat starts out played by you: you choose its roster from your own lists and record its side. To let the guest play on their own phone, choose **Invite to play** beside their name. Show them the QR code or use **Copy link** or **Share link**.

![The Invite to play dialog with a QR code, the seat link and a Copy link button.](screenshot:track-a-battle-guest-invite "Copy link, under the guest's QR code")

- The first device to open the link and choose **Join on this device** takes the seat for 30 days. No account is needed.
- The guest builds or imports a list in the roster builder on their device, then picks **Bring this roster** in setup.
- Once they've joined, their prompts and secret cards are theirs alone.
- If they lose their phone or switch device, open **Their device** and choose **Take back [name]'s seat**. You play their side again, and a fresh link appears for them.
