Paste a list from another army builder and Praetorium rebuilds it with its own points and datasheets. You'll always see a review first, and nothing is saved until you accept it. You don't need an account.

## Import a list

1. In the other app, use its text export and copy everything from the list title down. The faction, detachment and battle size lines decide which army and battle size you get.
2. Open [Rosters](/rosters) and choose **Import roster**.
3. Paste into **Roster text or exchange code** and choose **Import pasted roster**.
4. Read the [review](/docs/import-review), then accept it.

![The Import roster dialog with an official-app Necrons export pasted into Roster text or exchange code.](screenshot:import-a-roster-paste 'Import pasted roster')

Stuck? The **Import guide** help button at the top of the dialog walks you through it.

If you're not signed in, you get a draft kept on this device. **Save roster** takes you through signing in to keep it.

## Import from BattleBase or New Recruit

You can bring in several lists at once from these apps.

1. Open [Rosters](/rosters) and choose **Import roster**.
2. For **BattleBase**, enter a username and choose **Find**. You get that player's public rosters, so make a private roster public on BattleBase first. For **New Recruit**, paste one or more share links, one per line, and choose **Find lists**.
3. Lists that matched completely are ticked. Choose **Import 1 roster** or **Import 2 rosters**, whichever it says, to save them.
4. A list with anything to check shows **Review**. **Back to the lists** returns to the others.

## What you can import

- **Praetorium** text exports, exchange codes and QR links
- **New Recruit** text exports, including WTC-style and Markdown, and share links
- **ListForge** detailed, short and WTC text
- **BattleBase** text exports
- **The official Warhammer 40,000 app** text export, including other languages such as Spanish or German
- **Warmind** text, including ListForge-style, WTC and official-app-style
- **WarOrgan** text
- **Ironbuilt** text
- **Warscribe** text, in its GW and Compact layouts and as a Discord code block. Its share links can't be read, so copy the text instead.
- **40k.app** text and Markdown exports. Your notes on units become your private unit notes. Its list links can't be read, so copy the text instead.
- Other WTC-style tournament text
- Links from other sites that send a list here, which open the same review

If the lines above the units were cut from an official-app style export, the army is worked out from its units. Anything left above them shows in the review, and you choose the detachment in setup.

A list can hold at most 100 entries. Anything else is refused with "it is not a supported army-text export". `.ros`, `.rosz` and `.regiztry` files aren't supported yet, so use the app's text export instead.
