Paste a list from another army builder, or an exchange code from another Praetorium player, and Praetorium rebuilds it against its own points and datasheets. You always see a review first, and nothing is saved until you accept it.

## What you can import

- **Praetorium** text exports and exchange codes (including QR links)
- **New Recruit** text exports, including its WTC-style layout, and share links
- **ListForge** detailed text and WTC text
- **BattleBase** text exports
- **The official Warhammer 40,000 app** text export, including translated exports such as German
- **Warmind** text, including its ListForge-style, tournament (WTC) and official-app-style texts
- Other WTC-style tournament text

`.ros`, `.rosz` and `.regiztry` roster files aren't supported yet. Pasting or dropping one tells you so; use the app's text export instead.

A list can hold at most 100 entries. Anything else you paste is refused with "it is not a supported army-text export".

## Import a list

You do not need an existing roster or an account. Choose **Import an existing army** in **Getting started**, or the **Import guide** help button at the top of the import dialog, for help with pasting, reviewing, and opening your list. A visitor opens a draft kept on this device; **Save roster** then takes you through signing in to keep it in your account.

1. In the other app, use its text export and copy the whole thing, from the list title down. Keep the faction, detachment and battle size lines; they decide which army and battle size you get.
2. Open [Rosters](/rosters) and choose **Import roster**.
3. Paste into **Roster text or exchange code** and choose **Import pasted roster**.
4. Read the review, then accept it.

## Import from BattleBase or New Recruit

Bring in several lists at once from BattleBase or New Recruit, without copying their text.

1. Open [Rosters](/rosters) and choose **Import roster**.
2. Choose where the lists are:
   - **BattleBase**: enter a username and choose **Find**. You get that player's public rosters, yours or anyone else's.
   - **New Recruit**: paste one or more share links, one per line, and choose **Find lists**. Lists in another language are read too.
3. Lists that matched completely are ticked. Choose **Import rosters** to save them all.
4. A list with anything to check shows **Review** instead. It opens the usual review; **Back to the lists** returns to the others.

Private BattleBase rosters can't be found; make a roster public on BattleBase first. A New Recruit share link pasted into **Roster text or exchange code** opens the same way.
