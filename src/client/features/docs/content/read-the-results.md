## Read the results

The bar along the bottom updates as you change anything. It shows **Shooting + melee**: the **chance to destroy** the defender, the **avg. wounds lost** and the **avg. models lost**.

Open **Breakdown** to compare phases:

- **Shooting** and **Melee** each start from the defender's current health, as if that phase happened alone.
- **Combined** carries the survivors of shooting into the melee.
- Hover or tap a wounds or models figure to see the chance of at least each number.

![The results bar with Breakdown open, showing Shooting, Melee and Combined rows.](screenshot:compare-loadouts-breakdown 'Combined carries shooting survivors into melee')

With a **Command Re-roll** chosen, the bar also shows how much it changes each phase's chance to destroy, wounds lost and models lost compared with not using it, so you can tell whether it's worth the CP.

A higher average doesn't always mean a better chance to wipe the unit. If you need the kill, compare the destruction chance.

When the attacker would usually destroy the whole target, you may see **uncapped kills** instead of models lost: the average models destroyed if the target had enough models to absorb every attack.

## Recent simulations and sharing

When you're signed in, **Recent simulations** keeps your latest settings for six pairings, on your account rather than the device. Opening one recalculates with current rules and roster contents. The home page shows your newest three.

The simulator's address updates as you go, so copying the link shares the units, loadouts and settings. A side taken from a roster is linked by roster, not copied, so whoever opens it needs to be able to see that roster. If it has since been deleted or the unit removed, that side opens empty with a notice.
