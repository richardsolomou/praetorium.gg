The bar along the bottom updates as you change anything. **Shooting + melee** shows the **chance to destroy** the defender, the **avg. wounds lost** and the **avg. models lost**.

A higher average doesn't always mean a better chance to wipe the unit. Only the destruction chance tells you that.

If the attacker would usually destroy the whole target, you may see **uncapped kills** instead of models lost. That's the average models destroyed if the target had enough models to absorb every attack.

With a **Command Re-roll** chosen, the bar also shows how much it changes each figure compared with not using it.

## Compare phases

Open **Breakdown**. **Shooting** and **Melee** each start from the defender's current health, as if that phase happened alone. **Combined** carries the survivors of shooting into the melee.

Hover or tap a wounds or models figure to see the chance of at least each number.

## Recent simulations and sharing

To share a simulation, copy the simulator's address. The link carries the units, loadouts and settings.

A side taken from a roster needs whoever opens the link to be able to see that roster. If it's been deleted or the unit removed, that side opens empty with a notice.

When you're signed in, **Recent simulations** keeps your latest settings for six pairings on your account. Opening one recalculates with current rules and roster contents. The home page shows your newest three.
