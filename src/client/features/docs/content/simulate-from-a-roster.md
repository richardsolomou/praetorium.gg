For a first comparison without a saved roster, open [Combat simulator](/simulator) and choose **First matchup guide**. Choose an attacker and a defender, then read the results; equipment, buffs, and other conditions remain available when you need them. This also works without an account.

## From a roster, battle or tournament

**Simulate combat** on a selected unit in the roster builder opens the simulator with that unit, keeping its detachment, enhancements and attachments. Changes there are experiments: nothing is saved to the list, and closing discards them. On someone else's roster, it opens the main simulator with their unit defending, so you can attack it with one of your own.

In a battle, **Simulate** on a player's army opens the simulator with each unit's current models and wounds. Destroyed, reserve and embarked units can't be picked. Change the model count or **Wounded model · wounds left** to try a different state. If losses could have come from different models, **Survivors** lets you choose who's left.

In a tournament, once armies are revealed and you've submitted your own list, **Simulate** beside another entrant's army compares the two lists. In a doubles or 2v1 event, **Simulate** sits on the other pair or side and compares your side's lists against theirs.

## Offline

Once Praetorium has loaded on your device it keeps the army data it needs, so the simulator and Optimize keep working without a connection. Recent simulations you open or remove offline sync when you're back online.
