Post your group's or tournament's news to a channel on your Discord server, so players there see it without opening Praetorium. Each post is a line or two with a link back to the page.

## What gets posted

A **group** posts its league's results as each game finishes, a reminder about a day before each league round's deadline, and each hidden-list game's reveal and results.

A **tournament** posts the reveal, each Swiss round's tables with the standings from the rounds before it, and results as each game finishes.

Nothing posts before the reveal, and no post shows a list. A post names players and scores, which anyone who can open the linked page sees anyway. Posts never mention anyone in Discord.

## Connect a channel

You need permission to manage webhooks on the Discord server.

1. In Discord, open the channel's settings, then **Integrations**, then **Webhooks**.
2. Create a webhook, or choose one, and press **Copy Webhook URL**.
3. In Praetorium, choose **Edit group** or **Edit tournament**, paste the link under **Discord channel** and press **Save changes**.

Praetorium checks the link with Discord before it saves it. Once saved, the link isn't shown again, because anyone with it can post to your channel. The field names the webhook instead. A hidden-list game posts to its group's channel.

## Stop or change it

To stop posting, press **Stop posting** under **Discord channel** and save. To post somewhere else, paste a new link and save.

If Discord stops taking posts, for example because the webhook was deleted, posting stops and the field says so. Paste the link again, or a new one, to start again.
