Select a unit card to open its loadout. Along the top are:

- **Points** for the unit as built.
- **Model count**: use the minus and plus buttons, or pick a count from the number between them. Only sizes the datasheet allows are listed.
- **Warlord**: the first eligible unit you add becomes your Warlord automatically. Press **Warlord** on another character to move it; if you remove it on purpose, it stays removed.
- **Simulate combat**, which opens the [simulator](/docs/set-up-a-matchup) with this unit and never changes the list.

![An Intercessor Squad loadout with its points, model count and buttons along the top.](screenshot:build-an-army-loadout 'The loadout header: points, model count, Simulate combat and the full datasheet link')

Below, each kind of model has its own card with its weapons and choices, and choices for the whole unit sit under **Wargear options**. When a plus is disabled, its label says why: **Max 2** means that option is at its cap, and **Group full: 3/3** means the group's other options have used the room.

Enhancements and detachment **Unit upgrades** are chosen here too, with **No enhancement** or **No upgrade** to clear them. A chosen enhancement shows as a chip on the unit's card.

**Custom name** renames the unit, up to 40 characters, so you can tell two copies of the same datasheet apart. Its card shows the custom name above the datasheet name, and anyone who can read the roster sees it, including your opponent in a battle and the text export.

The **Note** box holds up to 500 characters that only you can see. Exports and other readers never get it.

**Starts embarked in** appears when the roster has a transport. Choose the transport the unit starts in, or **Not embarked**. When you attach the roster to a battle, the unit starts in that transport, and an attached Leader goes in with it. Battle setup checks the transport's capacity and keywords. If the unit does not fit, it starts on the battlefield. You can still change the choice at the **Reserves** step.

## Unit actions

Each card's menu (or a right-click) offers **Duplicate unit**, **Save loadout** and **Delete unit**. **Save loadout** keeps the model count and wargear under a name, up to 100 per account. It then appears beneath its datasheet in the picker for any roster of the same army; enhancements, upgrades, Warlord and attachments aren't kept.

## Attach Leaders and Support units

Attach from the roster cards, not the picker:

- On a Leader or Support unit, **Attach to unit** lists the units it can join.
- On a Bodyguard unit, **Attach unit** lists what can join it.

Each menu has **In this roster** and **Add a new unit**. The second adds the datasheet and attaches it in one step, with its price shown. Units at their copy limit are greyed out with the reason. Once joined, the card shows **Leading** or **Supporting** with **Remove**, and the Bodyguard shows **Leader** or **Support** with **Detach**. A Leader may stand on its own, but a Support unit must be attached to a Bodyguard.
