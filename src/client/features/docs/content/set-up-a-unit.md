Select a unit card to open its loadout. Use the minus and plus buttons to change the **Model count**, or pick from the number between them. Only sizes the datasheet allows are listed. At the largest size, the most it allows shows under the plus button. **Simulate combat** opens the [simulator](/docs/set-up-a-matchup) with this unit and never changes the list.

## Wargear and enhancements

Each kind of model has its own card with its weapons and choices. Choices for the whole unit are under **Wargear options**. If a plus is disabled, its label says why: **Max 2** means that option is at its cap, and **Group full: 3/3** means the group's other options have used the room. Wargear the datasheet gives to all models or none, such as a Combat Shield on every Vanguard Veteran, has a single plus that equips the whole squad.

Pick enhancements and detachment **Unit upgrades** here too. **No enhancement** or **No upgrade** clears them.

## Warlord

The first eligible unit you add becomes your Warlord. To move it, press **Warlord** on another character. If you remove it on purpose, it stays removed. A unit whose datasheet says it must be your Warlord, such as Trajann Valoris, takes it as soon as you add it and keeps it.

## Names and notes

**Custom name** renames the unit, up to 40 characters, so you can tell two copies apart. Anyone who can read the roster sees it, including your opponent in a battle and the text export.

The **Note** box holds up to 500 characters that only you can see. Exports and other readers never get it.

## Unit actions

Open a card's menu, or right-click it, for **Duplicate unit**, **Save loadout** and **Delete unit**.

**Save loadout** keeps the model count, wargear and custom name, up to 100 per account. It appears beneath its datasheet in the picker for any roster of the same army. Enhancements, upgrades, Warlord and attachments aren't kept.

A unit with characters attached also offers **Save attached unit**. It gets its own row in its bodyguard's section of the picker, and adding it brings the units back already attached.

## Attach Leaders and Support units

You attach from the roster cards, not the picker.

1. On a Leader or Support unit, choose **Attach to unit**. On a Bodyguard unit, choose **Attach unit**.
2. Pick a unit under **In this roster**, or use **Add a new unit** to add the datasheet and attach it in one step.

Units at their copy limit are greyed out with the reason. A Leader may stand on its own, but a Support unit must be attached to a Bodyguard.

![An Ancient's Attach to unit menu listing an Intercessor Squad in the roster and new squads with their prices.](screenshot:build-an-army-attach 'The Attach to unit menu, with In this roster and Add a new unit')

To undo it, use **Remove** on the card showing **Leading** or **Supporting**, or **Detach** on the Bodyguard. A joined unit's menu (**⋮** or right-click) also has **Detach from unit**.

An attached unit sits on its Bodyguard's shelf, which counts it as one unit. To put each card back on its role shelf, untick **Group attached units** under **Display** in the roster's menu. That's saved on this device for every roster.

To read a long list at a glance, tick **Show as list** under **Display** in the roster's menu. Each unit, or each attached unit, takes one line: its model count, name, any enhancement or upgrade, the transport it starts in, and its points. Choose a name to open its loadout. Untick it to see wargear or attach and embark units. The choice is saved on this device for every roster.

Supported unit-wide weapon grants appear on the attached unit's weapon profiles automatically, including Alpha Warrior's **Sustained Hits 1**. Detaching the source removes its grant from the Bodyguard's profiles.

## Start units embarked

You embark from the roster cards too. On a unit, choose **Embark in transport**. On a transport, choose **Embark unit**. An attached unit has **Embark unit in transport** below its cards. A transport the unit can't fit in, or whose capacity can't be read, is greyed out with the reason.

Once aboard, the unit's card shows **Embarked in** (or **Unit embarked in** for an attached unit) with **Remove**, and the transport has **Disembark**. To move a unit to another transport, remove it first. A Leader or Support unit embarks with the unit it joined.

If a change means a unit no longer fits, such as a bigger squad or a Leader joining it, it goes back to the battlefield and a brief message says why. If the transport itself changes, the units added to the roster last go back first.

Battle setup starts the unit in that transport, and you can still change it there.

## Rules you choose during the battle

Some rules put a unit in one of several options during the battle, such as the **Doctrina Imperatives** or the **Battle Protocols** a Cybernetica Datasmith gives Kastelan Robots. Each one the unit has is listed under its characteristics with its options. Pick an option to see the characteristics and weapons as it leaves them, including abilities like **Assault** that change no dice roll. Pick it again to clear it.

When another rule makes several options active at once for the unit, such as **Cognitive Reinforcement** making both Doctrina Imperatives active, it shows **Both** with that rule's name. If that rule has a condition to check first, it's offered as an extra option instead.

A unit that a rule lets hold more than one option at once, such as a unit led by Ursula Creed with two Orders, takes that many picks; a further pick replaces the oldest. A rule that gives the unit an option of its own on top, as Warrior Elite does for Kasrkin, adds one more pick, so Kasrkin led by Ursula Creed take three Orders. A rule that adds an option to every other, as Stalwart's Honours adds **Take Cover!**, applies it with whichever you pick.

A choice the whole army shares, like a Doctrina Imperative, follows you from unit to unit; any other, like an Order, stays with the unit you picked it for. An attached unit is one unit, so a pick on its Bodyguard or any character joined to it applies to all of them, and taking it back removes it from all of them. Some army choices only reach the units you select for them, like the Override **Noospheric Transference** gives: pick it on each unit you select, up to the number your battle size allows. Every selected unit shares one Override, so picking another on any of them changes it for all; picking a unit's Override again deselects only that unit. Picks are kept for each list, with your account once you're signed in and in this browser otherwise, and show the same in the builder, on the list's page and in a battle's units, and a unit's own picks clear when you add, remove or move a unit. Picks never change the list, and conditions such as being near a Battleline unit are left for you to check. A rule whose options Praetorium can't calculate, such as **Combat Doctrines**, still lists them so you can keep track of your pick, but they don't change the characteristics or weapons. Rules Praetorium can't read at all stay in the unit's abilities.

## Ability alerts

Press the bell beside an ability, rule or chosen enhancement, then choose when the alert appears, such as the start of your Shooting phase. The alert suggests when the rule acts, for you to check.

An alert covers every unit with that ability in all your rosters, and in battle one reminder names them all. You can keep up to 200. Signed in, your account keeps them. Otherwise this browser does, and saving your list to an account brings them along.

A battle keeps the alerts its armies started with, so changing one later doesn't change a battle under way. Sealing a list doesn't seal your alerts, so a tournament or group battle uses the alerts you have when it starts. Alerts for phases that pass while the battle isn't open show when you return to it on the same device. An alert due while you're scoring, drawing secondaries or answering another prompt waits until you've finished it. An ability that differs between rules versions may need its alert set again.
