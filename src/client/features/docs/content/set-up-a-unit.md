Select a unit card to open its loadout. Along the top are:

- **Points** for the unit as built.
- **Model count**: use the minus and plus buttons, or pick a count from the number between them. Only sizes the datasheet allows are listed.
- **Warlord**: the first eligible unit you add becomes your Warlord automatically. Press **Warlord** on another character to move it; if you remove it on purpose, it stays removed. A unit whose datasheet says it must be your Warlord, such as Trajann Valoris, takes it as soon as you add it and keeps it. Your Warlord has a crown beside its name in the roster list.
- **Simulate combat**, which opens the [simulator](/docs/set-up-a-matchup) with this unit and never changes the list.

![An Intercessor Squad loadout with its points, model count and buttons along the top.](screenshot:build-an-army-loadout 'The loadout header: points, model count, Simulate combat and the full datasheet link')

Below, each kind of model has its own card with its weapons and choices, and choices for the whole unit sit under **Wargear options**. When a plus is disabled, its label says why: **Max 2** means that option is at its cap, and **Group full: 3/3** means the group's other options have used the room.

Enhancements and detachment **Unit upgrades** are chosen here too, with **No enhancement** or **No upgrade** to clear them. A chosen enhancement shows as a chip on the unit's card.

**Custom name** renames the unit, up to 40 characters, so you can tell two copies of the same datasheet apart. Its card shows the custom name above the datasheet name, and anyone who can read the roster sees it, including your opponent in a battle and the text export.

The **Note** box holds up to 500 characters that only you can see. Exports and other readers never get it.

## Ability alerts

The bell beside an ability, rule or chosen enhancement sets an alert for it. Choose when it appears, such as the start of your Shooting phase; the alert suggests a time from the rule's wording for you to check. An alert belongs to you rather than the roster: it covers every unit with that ability in all your rosters, and each unit holding it gets its own alert in battle. An army or detachment rule alerts once for the whole army. The unit's card counts the alerts it will give. You can keep up to 200 alerts.

Signed in, your account keeps your alerts; otherwise this browser does, and saving your list to an account brings them along. A battle keeps the alerts its armies started with, so changing one later doesn't change a battle already under way. Sealing a list doesn't seal your alerts: a tournament or club battle uses the alerts you have when it starts. An ability whose catalogue entry differs between rules versions may need its alert set again.

## Unit actions

Each card's menu (or a right-click) offers **Duplicate unit**, **Save loadout** and **Delete unit**. **Save loadout** keeps the model count, wargear and custom name under a name, up to 100 per account. It then appears beneath its datasheet in the picker for any roster of the same army; enhancements, upgrades, Warlord and attachments aren't kept. A unit with characters attached also offers **Save attached unit**, which keeps every unit in it. The picker lists it as its own row in its bodyguard's section, and adding it brings the units back already attached.

## Attach Leaders and Support units

Attach from the roster cards, not the picker:

- On a Leader or Support unit, **Attach to unit** lists the units it can join.
- On a Bodyguard unit, **Attach unit** lists what can join it.

Each menu has **In this roster** and **Add a new unit**. The second adds the datasheet and attaches it in one step, with its price shown. Units at their copy limit are greyed out with the reason. Once joined, the card shows **Leading** or **Supporting** with **Remove**, and the Bodyguard shows **Leader** or **Support** with **Detach**. A joined unit's menu (**⋮** or right-click) also has **Detach from unit**. A Leader may stand on its own, but a Support unit must be attached to a Bodyguard.

An attached unit sits on its Bodyguard's shelf, which counts it as one unit; each shelf's heading shows the points its units cost. The Bodyguard comes first, then its Leaders and Support units indented under it, with their cards just saying **Leader** or **Support**. Below them is what the whole unit does: **Attach unit** while it has a free slot, and **Embark unit in transport**. To put each card back on its role shelf instead, untick **Group attached units** under **Display** in the roster's menu. The choice is saved on this device for every roster.

## Start units embarked

Embark from the roster cards too:

- On a unit, **Embark in transport** lists the roster's transports with how many models each already carries.
- On a transport, **Embark unit** lists the units that could ride in it.

A transport the unit can't fit in, or whose capacity can't be read, is greyed out with the reason. Once aboard, the unit's card shows **Embarked in** with **Remove** (an attached unit shows **Unit embarked in** below its cards), and the transport lists each unit inside with **Disembark**. To move it to another transport, remove it first. A Leader or Support unit embarks with the unit it joined, listed once alongside it. If a change to a unit means it no longer fits, such as a bigger squad or a Leader joining it, that unit goes back to the battlefield and a brief message says why. If the transport itself changes, the units added to the roster last go back first. Battle setup starts the unit in that transport, and you can still change it there.
