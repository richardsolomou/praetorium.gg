Points, datasheets and the app itself change often. Your rosters, battles and events don't change with them, and links you've shared keep working.

## Your rosters

- An update never deletes a roster or quietly removes a unit from it, and never swaps one unit or option for another.
- If an update renames a unit, option or detachment, your roster moves to the new name for you.
- If something leaves the game with no replacement, it stays in your roster and the list check marks it as no longer legal, so you decide what to do with it.
- A roster stays on the rules version you built it for. When an old version is retired, its rosters move to the current rules, and [Data updates](/data-updates) shows what changed.

## Battles and events

Played battles, and lists sealed for a tournament or hidden-list game, stay exactly as they were played: the same units, points and rules version, whatever changes later.

## Links and codes

- Links to rosters, battles, groups, leagues, tournaments, invites and stream overlays keep working unless their owner makes them private or deletes them. If a page moves, its old link takes you to the new one. A battle nobody else has joined is removed after 14 days without changes, together with its invite link.
- Your calendar link keeps working until you replace or turn it off.
- Every exchange code Praetorium has made can still be imported.

Text exported for other apps follows those apps' formats, so it can change when they do.

## Where the data comes from

Praetorium builds its data from community sources, listed on [Sources](/sources). If a source becomes unavailable, Praetorium moves to another one. Rosters, battles and events carry over as described above, and anything the new data doesn't cover yet is reported as unsupported instead of guessed.

## AI assistants

Connected assistants keep the tools they use. Tools gain new options over time, but an existing tool or option isn't removed without notice in [What's new](/whats-new).
