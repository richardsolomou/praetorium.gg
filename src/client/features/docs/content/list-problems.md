## Common problems

### Add is greyed out

You've reached that datasheet's limit, shown as "Limit reached: 3 of 3 permitted copies in the roster." If the data says what unlocks it, the message names it: allied Daemons, for example, read "Add a Khorne Battleline unit to the roster first." until the matching Battleline unit is in. "No copies available with the current detachments and prerequisite units." means the unit needs a different detachment or another unit first.

### A unit isn't in the picker

Check your filters ("Everything is filtered out." means they hide every match) and your detachments. For a Legends or Crucible datasheet, turn on **Legends** or **Crucible**.

### I can't select a detachment

A greyed card tells you why: "An army can include only one … detachment". If the dialog says the combination costs too many DP, drop one or move up a battle size.

### Attach to unit doesn't appear

Nothing in the list or the book can take that attachment. Support units still need a Bodyguard.

### "Your latest changes have not been saved."

Press **Try again** once you're back online.

### "This roster is locked. Unlock it to add units."

Unlock it from the roster menu, or create a variant. The longer message "This roster is locked. Unlock it to change it, or keep your changes as a new roster." has the same fix.

### A points value or datasheet looks wrong

Choose **Something wrong?** on the datasheet page, or in the unit's menu in the builder. Feedback opens with the datasheet, faction and rules version filled in, so you only need to say what looks wrong and what your source says. Nothing else from your roster is sent.

## Limits

- A roster holds at most 100 entries, and roster names are up to 80 characters.
- A clean footer doesn't promise a legal list. Anything under **Could not check every rule**, and your event's own restrictions, are yours to check.
- Lists already taken into a battle or tournament keep the version they were fielded with. Editing the roster afterwards doesn't change them.
