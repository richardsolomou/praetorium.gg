## Common problems

### Add is greyed out

You've reached that datasheet's limit, shown as "Limit reached: 3 of 3 permitted copies in the roster." "No copies available with the current detachments and prerequisite units." means the unit needs a different detachment or another unit first.

### A unit isn't in the picker

Check the filters ("Everything is filtered out." means they hide every match), your detachments, and, for a Legends or Crucible datasheet, whether **Legends** or **Crucible** is on.

### I can't select a detachment

A greyed card explains itself: "An army can include only one … detachment". If they're selectable but the dialog says the combination costs too many DP, drop one or move up a battle size.

### Attach to unit doesn't appear

Nothing in the list or the book can take that attachment. Support units still need a Bodyguard.

### "Your latest changes have not been saved."

Press **Try again** once you're back online.

### "This roster is locked. Unlock it to add units."

Unlock it from the roster menu, or create a variant.

### "This event keeps … on" or "This event doesn't allow Legends datasheets…"

A league event seals matched-play lists unless its organizer allows more under **House rules** in the event settings. The league page shows what the event allows beside its format, such as **Legends allowed · Restrictions off: Warlord**. Switch the restriction back on in the picker's **Format restrictions**, or remove the Legends or Crucible unit, then seal again. Events created before organizers could choose this accept every restriction a list switches off except the Warlord, plus Legends and Crucible.

## Limits

- A roster holds at most 100 entries, and roster names are up to 80 characters.
- A clean footer doesn't promise a legal list. Anything under **Could not check every rule**, and your event's own restrictions, are yours to check.
- Lists already taken into a battle or league keep the version they were fielded with; editing the roster afterwards doesn't change them.
