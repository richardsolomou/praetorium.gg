Praetorium checks your list as you edit. The footer shows your points against the limit and how many are left or over. It checks unit and detachment limits, units only a particular detachment allows, points caps such as the 500 points of Deathwatch a chapter may take, wargear, enhancements, attachments and Force dispositions that no detachment offers.

## When something's wrong

Problems appear in a red list in the footer, such as "must be attached to a Bodyguard unit" or "cannot lead Intercessor Squad, which is already led by…". The first eight are shown. If a unit holds choices its datasheet no longer offers, **Reset options** clears them.

![The roster footer showing 215/2000 points and the error "Ancient: must be attached to a Bodyguard unit".](screenshot:build-an-army-validation 'The red list of problems in the footer')

Detachment problems appear under the roster header with **Review setup**.

**Could not check every rule** lists restrictions Praetorium can't evaluate. They aren't marked legal, so check them yourself before playing.

## Switching restrictions off

For a house-rules game, open **Format restrictions**, the **⋮** beside the picker's search box, and untick what you don't want checked. It lists **Points limit**, **Detachment points** and **Warlord** (exactly one Character unit is your Warlord), plus the King of the Colosseum rules at that size. Its Toughness limit reads each datasheet's own Toughness, so a leader or enhancement that raises it during the battle doesn't count. A dot on the **⋮** shows that something is switched off, and hovering the warning triangle beside the points total says what. Everyone you play can see what your list waives.
