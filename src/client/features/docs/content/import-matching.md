## Model counts

When the text states a unit's models, those are used. When it doesn't, the count comes from the model lines the text names, then from the largest wargear count it states (for example `10x Tesla carbine`), and failing that the unit's minimum size. If the result isn't a size the datasheet allows, the review says what sizes it fields. Check squad sizes in the builder after importing.

## Characters and attachments

Leaders come across attached when the text says which unit they join. Praetorium's own text names it on a **Leading:** line; the official app and New Recruit group them under attached units, and each leader and support character joins the bodyguard in its group. WarOrgan lists a group without saying which unit is the bodyguard, so its characters join the one unit of the group they can lead.

If an attachment can't be made, the unit still imports but the review says why: "this list has no …" when the bodyguard unit isn't in the list, or "every … in this list is already led" when each unit of that name already has a leader. Attach it yourself in the builder. Praetorium's own rules then check that the attachment is allowed.

## Space Marine chapters

A list that names only Space Marines but fields a chapter's own units, such as Marneus Calgar, imports as that chapter, as long as its units belong to just one.

## Official-app exports in other languages

Translated official-app exports are read by their layout rather than their English words: the points in the title, the battle size, the attached-unit groups and the Warlord. Faction, detachment and force disposition names are matched through [New Recruit's community translations](https://nrdata.org), which cover French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Korean and Japanese to varying degrees. The app prints unit and wargear names in English, so those match as usual.

If a faction's name isn't translated yet, Praetorium takes the faction whose datasheets hold most of the list's units. A list built only from units several factions share, such as a Space Marine chapter list with no units unique to the chapter, can't be told apart that way, and the review says no faction matched. A detachment or force disposition with no translation appears in the review; a disposition is then taken from the matched detachments, and if they disagree the review asks you to choose one in setup.
