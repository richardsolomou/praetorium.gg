The gear button beside each member (**Loadout**) opens its wargear. Each option shows what it would do against the current defender, per phase: the chance to destroy the unit and the average wounds caused. The best option is tagged **Best kill %**; if kill chances tie, it's **Best models** or **Best wounds** instead, and hovering the tag explains why. Model-count options show the odds for a unit with one more or one fewer model.

![A Sergeant wargear option in the Loadout panel with its shooting and melee odds.](screenshot:compare-loadouts-best-kill 'Best kill % marks the option most likely to destroy the defender')

Every equipped weapon also appears under **Shooting** or **Melee** in the matchup. Use a weapon's switch to leave it out of that phase's attacks without changing the loadout. When a weapon has more than one mode, or a choice like **One Shot available** or **Already fired**, a dropdown above the weapons picks which applies.

## Optimize

The wand button next to the attacker's points (**Optimize**) searches the whole attached unit's legal wargear for the best chance to destroy the defender, with model counts held fixed. It ranks shooting followed by melee; the arrow beside it offers **Optimize for ranged** and **Optimize for melee** if you only care about one phase.

![The attacker card with the Optimize menu open.](screenshot:compare-loadouts-optimize 'Optimize, with Optimize for ranged and Optimize for melee in the menu beside it')

While it runs, the button shows a percentage; press it again to cancel and keep the improvements found so far. Afterwards a note may tell you:

- Not every loadout was checked, so the result is the best found, not a guaranteed best.
- Loadouts with rules the simulator cannot estimate were left out.

Optimize isn't available in a battle, because the units there are already fielded.
