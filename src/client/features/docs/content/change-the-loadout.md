To compare wargear, press the gear button (**Loadout**) beside a member. Each option shows, per phase, its chance to destroy the current defender and the average wounds caused.

The best option is tagged **Best kill %**. If kill chances tie, it's **Best models** or **Best wounds**. Model-count options show the odds with one more or one fewer model.

![A Sergeant wargear option in the Loadout panel with its shooting and melee odds.](screenshot:compare-loadouts-best-kill 'Best kill % marks the option most likely to destroy the defender')

Use a weapon's switch under **Shooting** or **Melee** to leave it out of that phase without changing the loadout. If a weapon has more than one mode, or a choice like **One Shot available** or **Already fired**, pick it from the dropdown above the weapons.

## Optimize

The wand button next to the attacker's points (**Optimize**) searches the whole attached unit's legal wargear for the best chance to destroy the defender. Model counts stay fixed.

It ranks shooting followed by melee. The arrow beside it offers **Optimize for ranged** and **Optimize for melee**.

![The attacker card with the Optimize menu open.](screenshot:compare-loadouts-optimize 'Optimize, with Optimize for ranged and Optimize for melee in the menu beside it')

Press the button again while it runs to cancel and keep the improvements found so far.

A note may appear afterwards. It may say not every loadout was checked, so a better one could exist, or that loadouts with rules the simulator can't estimate were left out.

Optimize isn't available in a battle, because the units there are already fielded. [Is it greyed out?](/docs/simulator-problems#optimize-is-greyed-out)
