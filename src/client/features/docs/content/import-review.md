The review shows how your list came across before anything is saved. If everything matched, choose **Import roster**, or **Open imported draft** if you're not signed in.

## Reading the review

The top line shows how many entries matched, such as "9 of 10 matched entries".

**Reported army** is the total your export stated, or "points not stated". **Matched entries at selected rules** is what the list costs in Praetorium today, and that's the price saved and checked. **Battle size** appears when the text named one.

![The import review for a fully matched list, showing Reported army: 1000 points and Matched entries at selected rules: 1000 points.](screenshot:import-a-roster-review 'Reported army and Matched entries at selected rules')

### Will not be imported

The import left these out, each with a reason:

- A unit with no matching datasheet. The reason may give the closest name ("the closest is …") or say it's another faction's ("a Drukhari datasheet, not …").
- A detachment that doesn't exist for the faction.
- A datasheet, detachment or enhancement a data update removed ("removed in the 30 Sept 2026 data update").
- A line the importer didn't understand.
- A battle size Praetorium doesn't support, or a custom points budget it can't accept.
- A force disposition that wasn't recognised, or more than one stated at once. Choose one in setup afterwards.

### Will arrive as their datasheets build them

These units imported, but some choices didn't, so they keep their datasheet defaults. Each line says **Could not apply** and why:

- "an enhancement of the … detachment": it belongs to a detachment the list didn't take.
- "this datasheet cannot take that enhancement" or "this datasheet does not offer it".
- "nothing in the … catalogue is called that": the name doesn't exist for this army.
- "no model in the imported unit carries it": none of the imported models can hold the option.
- "this datasheet has no Warlord option".
- A model count such as "4 models" with "this datasheet fields 5 to 10 models".
- An attachment that couldn't be made. See [Characters and attachments](/docs/import-matching#characters-and-attachments).

### Exported from older app data

The official app prints its data version at the bottom of an export. If that's older than Praetorium's and something didn't match, the review says so. Export the list again from the app.

### Accepting

If anything's listed, the button reads **Import anyway**. Missing units are left out and unmatched wargear uses the datasheet defaults, so check those units in the builder.

![The import review listing Necron Warrior under Will not be imported and Chronomancer's Veil of Darkness enhancement under Could not apply.](screenshot:import-a-roster-shortfall 'Import anyway, below the two lists')

**Edit pasted text** (or **Edit code**) lets you fix the text and try again. **Cancel** closes without importing.

## Points don't match the export

The two totals differ when the export predates a points change, or when something didn't match and was left out or fell back to defaults. Praetorium's figure is the one it checks and uses in battles. See [Data updates](/data-updates) for recent changes.
