## Fix a mistake

The arrow beside the phase button (**Undo latest action**) reverses your last action. Repeat it to step back further, even into an earlier turn. Undoing a mission draw asks first, and the cards go back to the deck.

Undo doesn't reverse casualties. Use the opposite control: return a model, heal a wound or **Bring back** a destroyed unit.

Forgot something? In a guided battle, **Battle options** › **Record a missed action** books a stratagem, victory points or the extra CP against an earlier phase of this round or the last. End-of-battle points can't be recorded late. A stratagem's **⋮** menu has **Record as missed…**, which opens the same dialog.

## Play on several devices

Every seated player's screen stays in step. If two people press at the same moment, one is told **Your opponent got there first. Try that again.** Drawing your secondary missions at random still goes through when your opponent acts at the same moment.

Seated players can keep going offline if they've opened the battle online once. Changes sync when you reconnect.

## End the battle

A battle finishes when the fifth round's scoring is done.

To stop early, open **Battle options** and choose **Concede battle**, or **Concede for [name]** for someone else. A concession is a loss for that side whatever the score. In a battle you play alone, only your own side can concede.

Only the person who created the battle can **Delete battle**. That removes it for everyone, for good.
