A club keeps a running leaderboard of the games your group plays. There's no end date: play each other whenever you like, and every game you play for the club goes on its leaderboard. Start one with **New club** on [Clubs](/clubs).

## The club page

**Up next for you**, beside the page on a computer and at its top on a phone, shows the one thing waiting on you: a battle you're playing, a list to seal, revealed lists to play, or your next season game.

The page has four tabs:

- **Overview**: the current sealed game, your unfinished battles, and the club's recent games. Each battle says whether it was a season game or a friendly game.
- **Leaderboard**: every game played for the club, rated.
- **Season**: the round robin, with its own table and fixtures.
- **Sealed games**: the current one and every past one, with their battles and lists.

## Set up a club

- **Who can find it**: **Public** lists the club on the clubs page. **Private link** keeps it to people you send the link to.
- **Who gets in**: **Anyone who joins**, or **You approve** each request. Requests wait at the top of the club page under **Asking to join**. Switching to **Anyone who joins** lets in everyone waiting.
- **Admins**: the owner picks them from the members in **Edit club**, in the club page's ⋯ menu. The members list marks the owner and admins. Admins can edit the club and manage members. Only the owner can remove an admin or delete the club.

Use **Copy invite link** on the club page to bring people in. Only members can see a private club's leaderboard.

## Play for the leaderboard

On the club page, press **New game**, pick an opponent, and press **Start battle**. Or, when you set up a battle anywhere else, pick the club under **Club**. You'll only see it when everyone in the battle is a member. Games with a guest or practice opponent, and manually tracked games, can't count. You can change the choice until the battle starts.

When the game ends, the result goes on the club's leaderboard. Ratings work as on the site-wide [leaderboard](/leaderboard): everyone starts at 1000, beating a higher-rated player gains more, a concession is a loss, and allies share their side's result. Club games still count on the site-wide leaderboard too.

## Sealed games

An organizer can press **New game**, then **New sealed game**, and pick its format: a duel, solo vs pair, or doubles. It shows under **Up next** on **Overview**, where everyone seals a list in secret and you reveal them together.

- **Duel**: any member seals a list. After reveal, use **Play** beside the person you’re playing that evening; nobody has to play every participant.
- **Solo vs pair** and **doubles**: pick your side with **Join**, then seal a list for it. Each pair chooses its Force Disposition and whose Warlord leads before the reveal. Afterwards, **Start battle** sets up the one battle between the sides.

Before the reveal, an organizer can press **Remind players** to send a notification to each player in the game who hasn't sealed a list yet, once a day.

After reveal, use **Simulate** beside another player’s list to compare your sealed armies. For team games, the simulator combines each side’s lists.

The results go on the club's leaderboard. Starting a new sealed game automatically moves the previous one to **Past sealed games**, on the **Sealed games** tab. A scheduled game also moves there after its scheduled day ends; an undated game moves after the day its lists were revealed. Days end at midnight in the timezone of the organizer who created the game. An undated game with unrevealed lists stays open until replaced.

Past games keep their results and any battles already underway, which you can still resume. Expand **Lists** to view or simulate revealed armies. You can’t start another battle or change lists in a past game. **Recent games**, on **Overview**, shows the club's other battles.

## Seasons

A season is a round robin: everyone who joins plays every other player once, one-on-one. A club runs one season at a time.

- **Start**: an organizer presses **Start a season** on the **Season** tab. Members press **Join season** while it's a draft. Only organizers see the draft fixtures. **Draw again** shuffles them, and the same draw stays until someone joins, leaves or it's drawn again.
- **Publish**: needs at least 3 players. After that nobody else can join, and the fixtures can't be drawn again. With an odd number of players, each player has one bye.
- **Play**: press **Start battle** on one of your fixtures. Each of you picks a list at setup. You can play rounds in any order. Fixture games also count on the club's leaderboard.
- **Deadlines**: an organizer can set a deadline for each round. A game not finished by then shows as **Overdue**. Nothing is scored for it.
- **Table**: a win or a bye is worth 3 points and a draw 1. Ties are broken by victory points, then by the points of everyone you've played. Every bye counts from the start.
- **Withdraw**: choose **Withdraw from season** in the **Fixtures** ⋯ menu. Your unplayed fixtures become void, and your finished games stay on the table. Leaving the club withdraws you too.

**End season**, in the same menu, removes the fixtures and table. Their battles stay.

## Leave or delete a club

**Leave club** takes you off the leaderboard. Your games still count for your opponents, and your results come back if you rejoin. The owner can't leave their own club. **Delete club** removes the club, its members, its leaderboard, its season and its sealed games. Its battles stay.
