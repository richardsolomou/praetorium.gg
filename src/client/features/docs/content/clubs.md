A club keeps a running leaderboard of the games your group plays. There's no end date: play each other whenever you like, and every game you play for the club goes on its leaderboard. Start one with **New club** on [Clubs](/clubs).

## Set up a club

- **Who can find it**: **Public** lists the club on the clubs page. **Private link** keeps it to people you send the link to.
- **Who gets in**: **Anyone who joins**, or **You approve** each request. Requests wait at the top of the club page under **Asking to join**. Switching to **Anyone who joins** lets in everyone waiting.
- **Admins**: the owner picks them from the members in **Edit club**, in the club page's ⋯ menu. The members list marks the owner and admins. Admins can edit the club and manage members. Only the owner can remove an admin or delete the club.

Use **Copy invite link** on the club page to bring people in. Only members can see a private club's leaderboard.

## Play for the leaderboard

On the club page, pick an opponent under **New game** and press **Start battle**. Or, when you set up a battle anywhere else, pick the club under **Club**. You'll only see it when everyone in the battle is a member. Games with a guest or practice opponent, and manually tracked games, can't count. You can change the choice until the battle starts.

When the game ends, the result goes on the club's leaderboard. Ratings work as on the site-wide [leaderboard](/leaderboard): everyone starts at 1000, beating a higher-rated player gains more, a concession is a loss, and allies share their side's result. Club games still count on the site-wide leaderboard too.

## Sealed games

An organizer can start one with **New sealed game** and pick its format: a duel, solo vs pair, or doubles. It shows under **Up next** on the club page, where everyone seals a list in secret and you reveal them together.

- **Duel**: any member seals a list, and after the reveal each of you plays everyone else who sealed, with **Play** beside their name.
- **Solo vs pair** and **doubles**: pick your side with **Join**, then seal a list for it. Each pair chooses its Force Disposition and whose Warlord leads before the reveal. Afterwards, **Start battle** sets up the one battle between the sides.

The results go on the club's leaderboard. Once you've played, or a newer sealed game starts, the game moves to **Past sealed games**. It shows the battles each game produced, and you can still play a game you missed there. **Recent games** shows the club's other battles.

## Leave or delete a club

**Leave club** takes you off the leaderboard. Your games still count for your opponents, and your results come back if you rejoin. The owner can't leave their own club. **Delete club** removes the club, its members, its leaderboard and its sealed games. Its battles stay.
