Your name, picture and profile page are public, and your email is private. Battles and your collection are public too, and each roster has its own access.

## Battles

Battles are public. Anyone can find and watch your past and live battles, tournament games included, and finished ones count on the leaderboard. Spectators can't change anything or see a face-down Secret Mission.

## Rosters

Each roster has its own access: **Private**, **Unlisted** (anyone with the link) or **Public** (listed on your profile and for anyone to find). New rosters start **Public** unless you change **Roster visibility** under **Roster defaults**. Anyone can copy a public roster into their own rosters.

Making a roster private again stops its link working, but the copies of it already played in battles or sealed in a tournament stay as they were.

## Collection

Your collection is public: anyone can see its datasheets, model counts, photos and wishlist on your profile.

## Ask about your data

The [privacy policy](/privacy) lists what Praetorium stores and who it's shared with. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion, email [privacy@praetorium.gg](mailto:privacy@praetorium.gg) from your account's address.

## Delete your account

1. At the bottom of Account settings, choose **Delete account**.
2. Enter your password under **Confirm your password**. If you only sign in with a provider, your recent sign-in confirms it instead.
3. Tick **I understand that my account and its battles cannot be recovered.** and choose **Permanently delete account**. **Keep account** backs out.

Deletion removes your profile and sign-in methods, rosters, saved loadouts, ability alerts, collection with its photos and wishlist, favourites, friendships, group memberships, tournament entries, tournaments you organise, groups you own (for every member: the group, its leaderboard and its hidden-list games), and every battle you played, including games other players were in. It can't be undone.

It can't reach copies other players have already made: rosters they copied, and anything they downloaded or captured.

If you can't sign in, the [Delete account](/delete-account) page explains how to ask by email instead.
