Your name, picture and profile page are public, and your email is private. The rest depends on settings in [Account settings](/profile) and on each roster's own access.

## Battles

**Battle privacy** sets **Who can watch** your past and live battles, league games included:

- **Anyone**, the default. Your games are listed with public games, and finished ones count on the leaderboard.
- **Friends**. Friends of any seated player can watch.
- **Players only**. Only the seated players can find or watch it.

The most private choice at the table applies to everyone in that battle. Spectators can't change anything or see a face-down Secret Mission. Your profile's battle record only shows games the reader is allowed to watch.

![The Battle privacy section with Anyone, Friends and Players only.](screenshot:account-battle-privacy 'Who can watch, under Battle privacy')

## Rosters

Each roster has its own access: **Private**, **Unlisted** (anyone with the link) or **Public** (listed on your profile and for anyone to find). New rosters start **Public** unless you change **Roster visibility** under **Roster defaults**. Anyone can copy a public roster into their own rosters.

Making a roster private again stops its link working, but the copies of it already played in battles or sealed in a league stay as they were.

## Collection

**Show collection on profile** is on by default, so anyone can see your collection's datasheets, model counts, photos and wishlist. Switch it off to hide them.

## Ask about your data

The [privacy policy](/privacy) lists what Praetorium stores and who it's shared with. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion, email [privacy@praetorium.gg](mailto:privacy@praetorium.gg) from your account's address.

## Delete your account

1. At the bottom of Account settings, choose **Delete account**.
2. Enter your password under **Confirm your password**. If you only sign in with a provider, your recent sign-in confirms it instead.
3. Tick **I understand that my account and its battles cannot be recovered.** and choose **Permanently delete account**. **Keep account** backs out.

Deletion removes your profile and sign-in methods, rosters, saved loadouts, collection with its photos and wishlist, favourites, friendships, league entries, leagues you organise, and every battle you played, including games other players were in. It can't be undone.

It can't reach copies other players have already made: rosters they copied, and anything they downloaded or captured.

If you can't sign in, the [Delete account](/delete-account) page explains how to ask by email instead.
