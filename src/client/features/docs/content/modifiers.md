**Rules & buffs** lists the rules on each side that the simulator can calculate for this matchup, including those from attached units, enhancements and the detachment. Always-on effects that only change a profile show **Stats applied**. Anything that depends on the table (a target, a range, a stratagem, a once-per-battle ability) starts off, and you switch it on when it's actually in effect. A rule marked **Check range** is an aura, so confirm the distance yourself.

**Manual modifiers** covers anything the rules don't, separately for **All**, **Shooting** and **Melee**:

- **Rolls**: hit and wound modifiers, re-rolls and critical thresholds.
- **Command Re-roll**: spend the stratagem once in that phase on a hit, wound, save or Attacks roll. The simulator re-rolls the first failed hit, wound or save roll that no other rule already re-rolled, taking weapons in order, or the first single-die Attacks roll below its average. Hit rolls for indirect fire can't be re-rolled. A damage roll, an Attacks roll of several dice, and saves when the defender's models or critical wounds need different saves aren't calculated, and that phase says so instead.
- **Stats**: BS or WS, S, A, AP and D.
- **Abilities**: Sustained Hits, Lethal Hits, Devastating Wounds and damage re-rolls.
- **Situation**: **Cover (−1 BS)** and **Half range** for shooting, **Charged** for melee, plus indirect fire and **Decline Lethal Hits** when a weapon has them.
- **Defender**: T and Sv changes, an invulnerable save, Feel No Pain, save re-rolls, **−1 Damage** and **Half damage**.

A phase tab overrides **All**. A chip that wouldn't change anything is dimmed, and hovering it says why. **Reset** clears every modifier and weapon choice.

## Check what's included

**Calculation uses**, under the modifiers, lists everything behind the current numbers for each phase: profile changes, active rules on both sides, the weapon choices, any weapons you left out, and your manual modifiers (or **No manual overrides**). Check it before trusting a surprising result.
