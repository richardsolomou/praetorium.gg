Switch on what's in effect in **Rules & buffs**, add anything else in **Manual modifiers**, then check **Calculation uses** to see what was counted. Both sections start open on a tablet or computer and closed on a phone.

## Rules & buffs

This lists the rules on each side the simulator can calculate for this matchup, including those from attached units, enhancements and the detachment. Each side groups them under **Abilities**, **Detachment** and **Stratagems**, with the source and any condition under each rule's name.

Always-on effects that only change a profile sit under **Stats applied**. Anything that depends on the table, like a target, a range, a stratagem or a once-per-battle ability, starts off. Switch it on when it's actually in effect.

A rule marked **Check range** is an aura, so confirm the distance yourself.

**Crushing Impact** appears under **Stratagems** for a **MONSTER** or **VEHICLE** that's attacking. Switched on, it rolls one D6 per point of the unit's highest Toughness before the fight, and each 5+ is a mortal wound on the target, up to 6. The mortal wounds your own unit takes on each 1 aren't shown.

## Manual modifiers

Choose **All**, **Shooting** or **Melee**, then pick from these. A phase tab overrides **All**.

Values you count up or down, like a hit modifier or a Feel No Pain roll, are steppers: press **−** or **+**, tap the value to pick one directly, or tap the name to put the unit's own value back.

- **Rolls**: hit and wound modifiers, and the rolls that score Critical Hits and Critical Wounds.
- **Re-rolls**: re-roll 1s or every failed roll, to hit and to wound.
- **Command Re-roll**: the stratagem, once in that phase, on a hit, wound, save or Attacks roll.
- **Stats**: A, BS or WS, S, AP and D, from −6 to +6. The weapon cards show the changed values.
- **Abilities**: Sustained Hits, Lethal Hits, Devastating Wounds and damage re-rolls.
- **Situation**: **Cover (−1 BS)**, **Half range**, **Charged**, plus indirect fire and **Decline Lethal Hits** when a weapon has them. Conversion weapons score Critical Hits on 4+ unless they're within half range.
- **Range**: on the Shooting tab, how many inches away the target is. Its steps are where one of the unit's weapons reaches its half range or its full range. Weapons that can't reach are left out and marked **Out of range**, and each weapon gets Rapid Fire, Melta and Conversion by its own half range. Setting a range replaces **Half range**, which treats every weapon as within half range.
- **Defender**: T and Sv, an invulnerable save, Feel No Pain, save re-rolls, **−1 Damage** and **Half damage**.

A dimmed chip, or a stepper with a dashed border, wouldn't change anything. Hover it to see why. **Reset** clears every modifier and weapon choice.

Modifiers stay on when you change either unit, so you can check one situation against several targets. Weapons you switched off stay off while the attacker is the same. Swapping attacker and defender starts the other direction with its own modifiers, since the roles change, and swapping back brings yours back.

## Shooting type

A unit that can shoot in more than one way gets a **Shooting type** choice under **Shooting**. It starts on **Normal**.

- **Normal, target engaged**: against a Monster or Vehicle that's engaged with your other units. [BLAST] weapons can't fire and every attack is at -1 to hit.
- **Assault**: the unit advanced this turn, so only its [ASSAULT] weapons fire.
- **Close-quarters**: the unit is engaged and shoots a unit it's engaged with. Monsters and Vehicles fire everything except [BLAST] weapons, at -1 to hit unless the weapon is [CLOSE-QUARTERS] or [PISTOL]. Everything else fires only those weapons.

When a shooting type costs a weapon -1 to hit, its card shows the roll it needs in place of BS, and hovering the number shows the printed value.

## How some rules are applied

**Command Re-roll** re-rolls the first failed hit, wound or save roll no other rule already re-rolled, taking weapons in order, or the first single-die Attacks roll below its average. Hit rolls for indirect fire can't be re-rolled. A damage roll, an Attacks roll of several dice, and saves when the defender's models or critical wounds need different saves aren't calculated, and that phase says so.

Rules that re-roll one hit or wound roll, such as the Adeptus Custodes **Magna Imperator**, work the same way: the first failed roll of that kind each phase. An attached unit gets it once. A rule that re-rolls one damage roll, such as the Gladiator Lancer's **Aquilon Optics**, spends it on the first damage roll a free re-roll of any damage roll would take (see below). A rule limited to attacks against some targets, such as **MONSTER** or **VEHICLE** units, applies only when the defender has one of those keywords.

Rules that re-roll any Damage roll, such as Fire Dragons' **Assured Destruction**, re-roll only when a fresh roll is expected to take more wounds off the model the attack is allocated to. A roll that already destroys that model is kept.

An enhancement or ability that makes both **Doctrina Imperatives** active, such as **Cognitive Reinforcement**, applies the Protector and Conqueror Imperatives together. The option you pick under **Doctrina Imperatives** then only says whether the conditions they state are met.

**Voice of Command** on an Astra Militarum unit picks the Order it's under. Check that an officer who can issue that Order to the unit is in range.

**Aspect Shrine Token** appears once an Aeldari Aspect Warrior unit's loadout takes a token. Pick the phase and whether it changes a hit or a wound roll. It changes the first failed roll of that kind made by a model that isn't a **CHARACTER** to an unmodified 6, spending one token in that phase. It can't be combined with a **Command Re-roll** or another single re-roll in the same phase.
