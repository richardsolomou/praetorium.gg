In a tournament everyone seals a roster in secret, the rosters open together at the reveal, and then the players play each other while the results build up. Start one with **New tournament** on [Tournaments](/tournaments). A [club](/docs/clubs) runs the same sealing as a sealed game, started from the club page.

## Set it up

- **Battle format** is **Duel** (1v1), **Solo vs pair** (2v1) or **Doubles** (2v2).
- **Pairing**, for a duel tournament, is **Open play**, where players choose who to play as often as they like, or **Swiss rounds**, where you pair each round and set how many there are.
- **Points** sets the size every roster is built to. In a 2v1 the solo player builds to it and each ally to half. In doubles every player builds to half.
- **House rules** says what lists may do beyond matched play. All off is matched play. Untick a restriction, such as **Points limit** or **Warlord**, to let lists switch it off. Tick **Legends** or **Crucible** to let lists include those datasheets. A list that needs anything you left off can't be sealed. Team tournaments always need a Warlord in every list, because allies choose whose leads.
- **I'm playing too** enters you as a player. Leave it off to run the tournament without a roster of your own.
- **More options** holds the tournament's **Details**, a **Player limit**, **Who can find it** (a private link, or listed on the tournaments page) and whether **Anyone who joins** gets in or you approve each player.

The format, points and house rules lock once anyone seals a roster. Everything else can change at any time from **Edit tournament**.

## Fill it

Share the invite link from **Invite players**, or use **Add friends** to enter friends directly. With approval on, answer the requests in the entrant list. **Remove entrant** takes a player out, and their sealed roster with them.

In a 2v1 give each player a solo or allied size and pair the allies. In doubles pair everyone into teams of two. Each team then chooses its Force Disposition and whose Warlord leads before the reveal. Changing a player's size or team clears the roster they sealed.

## Reveal and play

When everyone is in and sealed, **Reveal all rosters** opens every list at once and closes entry. Anyone playing can reveal, not only the organizer, and the reveal can't be undone. Before then nobody sees another list, the organizer included.

After the reveal, players start their tournament battles from the tournament page, and finished battles fill the results table. If a revealed roster has a mistake, press the unseal button on that player's roster card, then **Unseal roster**, so they can seal a corrected one. Battles already started keep the roster they began with.

## Swiss rounds

After the reveal, press **Pair round 1**. The first round is drawn at random. Each later round pairs players on the same score, top of the table first, and never pairs two players who have already met when any other pairing works. With an odd number of players, the lowest-placed player who hasn't had a bye sits the round out, and the bye counts as a win. **Pair round 2** and the rounds after it open once every table has finished its battle. Each table's missions and battlefield come from its two armies, as in any battle.

The results rank by match points (3 for a win or bye, 1 for a draw), then total victory points, then strength of schedule: the match points of everyone a player has faced.

To play again with new rosters, start a new tournament. A [club](/docs/clubs) keeps a standing table across all of them.

## Organizers

The owner can choose **Admins** from their friends in **Edit tournament**. Admins run the tournament like the owner but can't delete it. **Delete tournament** removes every entrant and sealed roster. Battles already started stay where they are.
