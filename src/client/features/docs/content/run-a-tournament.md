In a tournament everyone seals a roster in secret, the rosters open together at the reveal, and then the players play each other while the results build up. Start one with **New tournament** on [Tournaments](/tournaments). To see one before you commit, open the [sample tournament](/tournaments/sample), a made-up Swiss event shown from sealed lists to the final standings. A [group](/docs/groups) runs the same sealing as a hidden-list game, started from the group page.

## Set it up

- **Battle format** is **Duel** (1v1), **Solo vs pair** (2v1) or **Doubles** (2v2).
- **Pairing**, for a duel tournament, is **Open play**, where players choose who to play as often as they like, or **Swiss rounds**, where you pair each round and set how many there are.
- **Points total**, for a duel, plays a battle size at its own total, such as Incursion at 750 points. Leave it empty for the size's full points. Only rosters built to that total can be sealed.
- **Played on** and **Seal rosters by** are optional. Players see both in their own timezone. After the deadline nobody can seal or join, and anyone without a sealed roster sits the event out, so they no longer hold up the reveal. You can move either date until the reveal.
- **Points** sets the battle size. A duel's rosters are built at it. In a 2v1 or doubles every roster is built at it or a smaller size, unless it waives **Points limit** and your house rules allow that.
- **Set points per army**, under **Points** in a 2v1 or doubles, changes what each player's list may spend from the even split: the **Solo player** and **Each ally** in a 2v1, or **Each player** in doubles, such as 1,750 against 800 each. Leave a box empty for an even share of the battle size: the full size for the solo player, and half for everyone else. **Use an even split** goes back. Allies seal lists saved to the same points total.
- **Allies split their points**, in a 2v1 or doubles, lets each pair spend its points between them however they agree, such as 900 and 1,100 of 2,000. Each list is still built at the battle size or smaller, and the pair's lists don't need the same points total. A list may cost more than the points it was saved to, such as 1,010 of 1,000 beside an ally's 990, as long as the pair stays within its total.
- **House rules** says what lists may do beyond matched play. All off is matched play. Untick a restriction, such as **Points limit** or **Warlord**, to let lists switch it off. Tick **Legends** or **Crucible** to let lists include those datasheets. A list that needs anything you left off can't be sealed. Team tournaments always need a Warlord in every list, because allies choose whose leads.
- **I'm playing too** enters you as a player. Leave it off to run the tournament without a roster of your own.
- **More options** holds the tournament's **Details**, a **Player limit**, **Who can find it** (a private link, or listed on the tournaments page) and whether **Anyone who joins** gets in or you approve each player.

Once anyone seals a roster, the format, battle size, pairing and points total lock. Points per army, **Allies split their points** and house rules can still change until the reveal, as long as every sealed roster still fits them; otherwise ask its player to seal a roster that does. Everything else can change at any time from **Edit tournament**.

## Fill it

Share the invite link from **Invite players**, or use **Add friends** to enter friends directly. With approval on, answer the requests in the entrant list. **Remove entrant** takes a player out, and their sealed roster with them.

In a 2v1 give each player a solo or allied size and pair the allies. In doubles pair everyone into teams of two. Each team then chooses its Force Disposition and whose Warlord leads before the reveal. Changing a player's size or team clears the roster they sealed.

While rosters are still owed, **Remind players** under the reveal countdown sends a notification to each accepted player who hasn't sealed, on devices where they allowed notifications. It names no deadline time, so players check it on the tournament page, and it reveals nothing. You can send one reminder a day.

## Reveal and play

When everyone is in and sealed, **Reveal all rosters** opens every list at once and closes entry. Anyone playing can reveal, not only the organizer, and the reveal can't be undone. Before then nobody sees another list, the organizer included.

After the reveal, players start their tournament battles from the tournament page, and finished battles fill the results table. If a revealed roster has a mistake, press the unseal button on that player's roster row, then **Unseal roster**, so they can seal a corrected one. Battles already started keep the roster they began with.

Anyone who can see the revealed rosters and results can save them: **Export rosters** writes every list as it was sealed into one text file, and **Export CSV** saves the results table. A saved file can't be recalled later, even if who can see the event changes.

## Swiss rounds

After the reveal, press **Pair round 1**. The first round is drawn at random. Each later round pairs players on the same score, top of the table first, and never pairs two players who have already met when any other pairing works; when none does, it makes as few rematches as it can. With an odd number of players, the lowest-placed player who hasn't had a bye sits the round out, and the bye counts as a win. When a small field runs out of new opponents, two players meet again and their table is marked **Rematch**. **Pair round 2** and the rounds after it open once every table has finished its battle. Each table's missions and battlefield come from its two armies, as in any battle.

The results rank by match points (3 for a win or bye, 1 for a draw), then total victory points, then strength of schedule: the match points of everyone a player has faced.

Once you pair the next round, the results of earlier rounds are locked. Only you and your admins can then correct one. To correct a wrong result in any round, press the reopen button beside the table's score, then **Reopen result**. You don't need to have played at that table. The battle continues from its last turn, and its players correct the score and finish it again. Until they do, the table has no result in the standings. Rounds that are already paired stay the same.

If a player leaves or doesn't show, press the drop button on their roster row, then **Drop player**. They aren't paired again and can't rejoin, and their results stay in the table, including in their opponents' strength of schedule. If their table in the current round hasn't finished, their opponent takes it as a bye, so the next round can be paired. A battle already started at that table stays in the battle list but no longer counts for the round. Players can withdraw themselves the same way.

To play again with new rosters, start a new tournament. A [group](/docs/groups) keeps a standing table across all of them.

## Organizers

The owner can choose **Admins** from their friends in **Edit tournament**. Admins run the tournament like the owner but can't delete it. **Delete tournament** removes every entrant and sealed roster. Battles already started stay where they are.
