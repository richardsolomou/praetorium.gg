The setup page follows the order the rules play it, down a line between the two sides, with the side that opened the battle on the left on every device. Each step shows each side's answer on its own side of the line, and a choice you both share, such as the battlefield, on the line itself. A step stays open while it needs an answer and folds to its answer once it has one; press a step's name to open or fold it. Mission, Secondaries, Reserves, Deploy and Pre-battle rules start at a valid default (the missions your armies give you, Tactical cards, every unit on the battlefield), so their step number is a checkbox: tick it once the table is done with the step, which folds it on every device, and untick it to open it again. The open step's name and checkbox stay at the top of the screen while you scroll through it. On a phone, an open step shows one side at a time: tap the other side's tab at the top of the step, or **Show** at the bottom, to see their choices. Ticking a step takes you to the next one.

The bar at the bottom counts what's left (for example **2 left · Battlefield · First turn**), and **Start battle** unlocks when it reads **Ready**. Press the count to jump to the next step that needs an answer.

1. **Format.** The battle size, the mission pack and whether the battle is tracked guided or manually, plus the group when you share one. The size follows your rosters' formats once every army is in. In a duel both sides need the same points. In a 2v1 or doubles the biggest list sets the size, and every other list may be built at it or smaller. Each army spends an even share of the battle size, shown beside it. **Set points per army** under the battle size gives each side its own number, such as 1,750 against 800 each, and **Use an even split** goes back. Allies bring lists saved to the same points total. Imported text-only armies need you to choose the size yourself.
2. **Armies.** Choose **Choose roster** on each seat. You see your saved rosters built for the battle size, plus any **Lent by friends**. For a practice opponent you pick from your own lists. Press **Battle ready** under an army to get +10 VP at the start of the battle; allies only get it once every army on their side is battle ready. Each list names its own Warlord, so a side of allies presses the crowned Warlord under the army whose Warlord leads it.
3. **Mission.** Each side's primary comes from its Force Disposition against its opponent's. An allied side with more than one disposition on offer picks one. The mission pack is chosen alongside the battle size.
4. **Battlefield.** Pick one of the layouts shown for the matchup. It sets the deployment zones and the terrain for both sides. Open a layout to set up the table: the pieces are numbered in the order to put them down, and each is measured square from one corner to a piece already down, a deployment zone line, or the board edge. **Measure to** starts on other terrain, with the board edge used when no piece is nearer, so it works on a blank mat. Tick **Deployment lines** or **Mat ruler** if your mat has the deployment zones or inch marks printed on it, or **Published distances** to show the distances from the layout's published diagram. Each option draws its distances in the colour of its tick box.
5. **Defender.** Roll off at the table, then record who defends. The defender deploys first.
6. **Secondaries.** Each side picks **Tactical** (cards drawn during the battle) or **Fixed** (missions selected now and kept all game). Allies share one hand.
7. **Reserves.** Move units into strategic reserves or deep strike, or embark them in transports. Units your roster [starts embarked](/docs/set-up-a-unit#start-units-embarked) are already inside their transport; a note says so if the frozen army can't fit one, and that unit starts on the battlefield instead. Anything you leave alone starts on the battlefield.
8. **Deploy.** Set the armies up at the table, then tick the step.
9. **First turn.** Roll off and record who goes first.
10. **Pre-battle rules.** Resolve them at the table, tick the step if you like, then choose **Start battle** to open the first Command phase.

Manual battles skip the mission, battlefield, defender and secondaries steps.

Your armies are frozen when you attach them. Editing or deleting the saved roster afterwards doesn't change this battle.
