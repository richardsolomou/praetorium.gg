The setup page follows the order the rules play it. The bar at the bottom counts what's left (for example **2 left · Battlefield · First turn**), and **Start battle** unlocks when it reads **Ready**.

![The setup bar reading 2 left, Defender and First turn, beside a greyed-out Start battle button.](screenshot:track-a-battle-setup-bar 'Start battle, locked until nothing is left')

1. **Armies.** Choose **Choose roster** on each seat. You see your saved rosters built for the battle size, plus any **Lent by friends**. For a practice opponent you pick from your own lists. Press **Battle ready** under an army to get +10 VP at the start of the battle; allies only get it once every army on their side is battle ready. Each list names its own Warlord, so a side of allies presses the crowned Warlord under the army whose Warlord leads it.
2. **Battle size.** This follows your rosters' formats once every army is in. In a duel both sides need the same points. In a 2v1 or doubles the biggest list sets the size, and every other list may be built at it or smaller. Each army spends an even share of the battle size, shown beside it. **Set points per army** under the battle size gives each side its own number, such as 1,750 against 800 each, and **Use an even split** goes back. Allies bring lists saved to the same points total. Imported text-only armies need you to choose the size yourself.
3. **Mission.** Each side's primary comes from its Force Disposition against its opponent's. An allied side with more than one disposition on offer picks one. The mission pack is chosen alongside the battle size.
4. **Battlefield.** Pick one of the layouts shown for the matchup. It sets the deployment zones and the terrain for both sides. Open a layout to set up the table: the pieces are numbered in the order to put them down, and each is measured square from one corner to a piece already down, a deployment zone line, or the board edge. Use **Measure to** to choose whether to measure from other terrain and the deployment lines, with the board edge used when neither is nearer, or to show the distances printed on the layout's published diagram or coordinates for a mat with printed inch marks. Each option draws its distances in the colour of its tick box.
5. **Defender.** Roll off at the table, then record who defends. The defender deploys first.
6. **Secondaries.** Each side picks **Tactical** (cards drawn during the battle) or **Fixed** (missions selected now and kept all game). Allies share one hand.
7. **Reserves.** Move units into strategic reserves or deep strike, or embark them in transports. Units your roster [starts embarked](/docs/set-up-a-unit#start-units-embarked) are already inside their transport; a note says so if the frozen army can't fit one, and that unit starts on the battlefield instead. Anything you leave alone starts on the battlefield.
8. **Deploy.** Nothing to record.
9. **First turn.** Roll off and record who goes first.
10. **Pre-battle rules.** Resolve them at the table, then choose **Start battle** to open the first Command phase.

Manual battles skip the mission, battlefield, defender and secondaries steps.

Your armies are frozen when you attach them. Editing or deleting the saved roster afterwards doesn't change this battle.
