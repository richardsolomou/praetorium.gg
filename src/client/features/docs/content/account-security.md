Everything on this page is in [Account settings](/profile). Open the account menu and choose **Account settings**, or in the app, choose **Profile** on the **More** tab.

## Sign-in methods

**Sign-in methods** lists **Password** and each provider, marked **Linked** or **Available to link**. Choose **Link** beside a provider and sign in with it to link it. Any linked method then signs you into the same account.

- **Change** on the password row asks for your **Current password** and a **New password**. Changing it signs out every other device.
- If you've only used a provider so far, **Create password** adds email and password sign-in alongside it.
- **Unlink** and **Remove** only appear while another method would stay linked, so you can't lock yourself out.

### Verify your email

If your email isn't verified yet, a **Verify your email** box sits at the top of the section. Choose **Send verification email** and open the link it sends; Account settings then shows **Email address verified.** Until you verify, an Apple, Google, Discord or GitHub account with the same email can't sign you in. Link it here instead.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a code from an authenticator app to password sign-in. It needs a password, so create one first if you only use a provider.

1. Under **Two-factor authentication**, choose **Set up authenticator**.
2. Enter your password under **Confirm your password** and choose **Continue**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or open **Cannot scan the QR code?** for the setup address. Enter the app's current **Authenticator code** and choose **Verify and enable**.
4. Choose **Copy recovery codes** and keep the ten codes somewhere safe. Each works once, and Praetorium doesn't show them again. Then choose **I saved my recovery codes**.

![The Two-factor authentication section in Account settings, showing the authenticator app as not set up.](screenshot:account-two-factor 'Set up authenticator, under Two-factor authentication')

![The recovery codes shown after setting up an authenticator, with buttons to copy them and to finish.](screenshot:account-recovery-codes 'Copy recovery codes, above I saved my recovery codes')

Next time you sign in with your password, enter the six-digit code and choose **Verify and sign in**. **Use a recovery code** swaps in one of your saved codes. Tick **Trust this device for 30 days** to skip the code on that device. Signing in with a linked provider doesn't ask for a code.

To switch it off, choose **Turn off**, confirm your password and choose **Turn off two-factor authentication**. Your unused recovery codes stop working.

## Connected apps

**Connected apps** lists AI assistants and other apps you've allowed to read or change your rosters and battles. Choose **Revoke access** to disconnect one. See [Let an assistant use your account](/docs/assistant-account).
