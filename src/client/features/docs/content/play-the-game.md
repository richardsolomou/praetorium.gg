The main button ends the current phase: **End the command phase**, **End the movement phase** and so on, then **Pass the turn** from the end phase. The phase strip above it shows where you are and how long the phase has taken. On a phone, switch between **Your side**, **Battle** and **Opponent**.

## Scoring

In a guided battle, ending a phase where a card scores opens a scoring prompt. Press what the board actually paid on each card, or **Did not score (or chose not to)**. The next player's turn waits until the previous turn's scoring is finished. At the end of the fifth round, the final scoring prompt ends with **Finish battle**.

![The end-of-turn scoring prompt listing the primary and two secondary cards, each with its awards and a Did not score row.](screenshot:track-a-battle-scoring "Did not score (or chose not to), under a card's awards")

Tactical cards are drawn when a turn needs them; you can draw at random or select exact cards. At the end of your turn you can discard tactical cards, which gains 1 CP.

When a card asks you to pick objectives, they are named from the deployment: each player's **home**, the **centre** (on six-objective layouts, the centre pair is named by the side each sits on), an **expansion** nearer one player's zone, and a **flank** as near to both. Left and right are as seen from the zone of the player making the choice.

In a manual battle, each side has **−1**, **+1** and **+5** buttons for primary and secondary, and **−1** and **+1** for CP.

## Command points and stratagems

Guided battles give each side its Command phase CP automatically. **+1 CP** records your one extra command point for the round; once you've used it, the button reads **CP gain used**. Discarding a secondary for CP uses the same allowance.

Press a stratagem's cost button (for example **2 CP**) to use one. The **⋮** beside it lets you spend a different amount, for stratagems whose cost depends on the board. A greyed-out stratagem shows why on hover: wrong phase, wrong turn, not enough CP, or its use limit is already spent.

## Casualties

Open **Army** under a player's name to see their units. Remove models and wounds as they go, or mark a unit **Destroyed**. Units in reserves or transports show **Arrive** or **Disembark**. **Simulate** opens the [combat simulator](/simulator) with the units still standing.

## Notes, timer and reminders

**Battle note** records a rules call or house rule in the battle log, up to 500 characters. Anyone who can watch the battle can read notes. **Battle options** holds **Pause timer** (shared by the whole table), **Action reminders** for your own list's reminders, and the ways the battle ends.
