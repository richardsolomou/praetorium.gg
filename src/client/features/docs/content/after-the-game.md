## After the game

A finished battle shows the result, the battle events (filter to **CP only** to check command point spending) and a timeline you can scrub through turn by turn. Watchers can scrub a live battle; seated players get the replay once it's finished.

- Battles between accounts count in each player's record.
- The [leaderboard](/leaderboard) ranks recent finished, guided battles that anyone can watch. A concession counts as a loss, and allies share their side's result.
- A guest battle counts in your record, not on the leaderboard.
- Practice battles count nowhere.

### Guests keeping the game

When the battle finishes, the guest's device shows **Keep this battle**. They choose **Create an account** or **I already have an account**, then **Add to my record**. It then counts as a game between two accounts: in both records, on the leaderboard if it's public, and under their privacy setting. The account they use can't already be seated in that battle.

## Who can watch

Set **Who can watch** under **Battle privacy** in your account settings: **Anyone** (the default), **Friends** (friends of any seated player) or **Players only**. A battle uses the most private choice of anyone seated, and that applies to league games too. A guest's name shows wherever the battle does.
