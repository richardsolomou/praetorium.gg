A finished battle shows the result, the battle events and a timeline you can scrub through turn by turn. Filter to **CP only** to check command point spending, and select a stratagem's name to read what it does. Watchers can scrub a live battle, and seated players get the replay once it's finished.

Battles between accounts count in each player's record. Practice battles count nowhere.

## Your opponent

Under the result of a battle between accounts, you see your record against your opponent so far, with a link to it on your profile. Practice battles show no record.

If an account you played isn't your friend yet, you can add them, or accept their request, from there, so you can start battles with each other later.

## The leaderboard

The [leaderboard](/leaderboard) rates everyone on every guided battle they've finished. Until you've finished 5 battles counted in a table, you're listed as not yet ranked. A rating drifts down while you don't play.

A concession counts as a loss, and allies share their side's result. In a team battle, each side is rated on its players' average rating, so beating two players together counts like beating one player of that strength. Each army has one table, whatever rules version you played it under.

Your battle list, your profile and every finished battle show how far each counted battle moved each player's rating. Your profile also charts your overall rating.

## Achievements

Every profile has an **Achievements** tab, with the player's latest three medals at the top. Tap or hover a medal to see what earns it. Bronze medals are worth 10 points, silver 25, gold 50 and platinum 100, and the total shows under the player's name.

- Battle achievements only count battles that count for the leaderboard, so guest and practice battles never earn them. A conceded win is never a close one, and a streak ends at any loss or draw.
- **Recruiter** went to the player whose guest created an account to add the battle to their record. Battles no longer seat guests, so it can't be earned any more, and anyone who earned it keeps it.
- Group achievements count casual battles played for a group and the group's hidden-list games.
- Tournament achievements need a Swiss tournament played to its last round.
- **Admired** counts likes from other players on one collection photo.
- Some medals count private things like rosters, friends and simulations. The medal shows you reached the count, never the details.
- Painting medals count models your collection marks painted, so they show no date.
- Undo or delete the battle that earned an achievement and it goes too.

## Who can watch

Anyone can find and watch a battle, tournament games included. A guest's name shows wherever the battle does.

Until a battle begins, watchers see its setup instead: each side's army, mission and other choices so far, and what is still to do.

Public and friends' games list the most recently played first. A battle nobody has started leaves those lists two days after its last change and returns once it begins. Its players still find it under their battles, and anyone with its link can still watch it.

A faction's page and its datasheet pages show a few of the latest finished public games that field that faction. The section is not shown when there are none.

## Show the score on a stream

To show a battle's score on a stream, choose **Battle options** > **Copy stream overlay link**, or add `/overlay` to the end of the battle's address. Add the link to your streaming software as a browser source. The overlay shows each side's names, armies, VP, CP and active secondary missions, and the round, phase and turn. It updates while you play. It has no background, so only the score strip covers your video. A secret mission stays face down until it is revealed.
