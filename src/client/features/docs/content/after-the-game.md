## After the game

A finished battle shows the result, the battle events (filter to **CP only** to check command point spending) and a timeline you can scrub through turn by turn. Watchers can scrub a live battle; seated players get the replay once it's finished.

- Battles between accounts count in each player's record.
- The [leaderboard](/leaderboard) lists everyone who finished a guided battle in the last 90 days. Your rating counts every guided battle you've finished, not only recent ones. A concession counts as a loss, and allies share their side's result. In a team battle, each side is rated on its players' average rating, so beating two players together counts like beating one player of that strength. Each army has one table, whatever rules version you played it under. Your battle list, your profile and every finished battle show how far each counted battle moved each player's rating, beside their name. Your profile also charts your overall rating after every counted battle.
- A guest battle counts in your record, not on the leaderboard.
- Practice battles count nowhere.

### Guests keeping the game

When the battle finishes, the guest's device shows **Keep this battle**. They choose **Create an account** or **I already have an account**, then **Add to my record**. It then counts as a game between two accounts: in both records and, if it was guided, on the leaderboard. The account they use can't already be seated in that battle.

## Who can watch

Anyone can find and watch a battle, tournament games included. A guest's name shows wherever the battle does.
