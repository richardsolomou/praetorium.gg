## Common problems

### "Enter your email first."

**Forgot password?** needs to know which account to reset. Type your email into **Email**, then choose **Forgot password?** again.

### The reset email never arrives

Check your spam folder and the address you typed; the page gives the same answer whether or not that account exists. If you've only ever used Apple, Google, Discord or GitHub, sign in with that instead.

### "This password reset link is invalid or has expired."

Reset links last an hour and work once. Choose **Return to sign in** and request a new one.

### "An account already uses this email. Sign in with its existing method, then link this provider from your profile."

You signed up with a password and that email isn't verified yet. Sign in with your password, then either link the provider under **Sign-in and security** or verify your email. See [Sign-in methods](/docs/account-security#sign-in-methods).

### "Could not sign in with this provider. Try again."

Use the same Apple, Google, Discord or GitHub account you originally linked, then try again.

### "This provider is already linked to another Praetorium account."

That provider account already signs in to another Praetorium account. Sign in with it to reach that account, or link a different one.

### "That authenticator code is invalid."

Codes depend on your phone's clock. Check that the date and time on the phone with your authenticator are set automatically, then enter the current code.

### I've lost my authenticator

Choose **Use a recovery code** and enter one of the codes you saved during setup. If you've used them all, sign in with a linked Apple, Google, Discord or GitHub account, which doesn't ask for a code, and turn two-factor off from [Account settings](/profile). Otherwise, email [privacy@praetorium.gg](mailto:privacy@praetorium.gg) from your account's address.

### Set up authenticator is greyed out

It says **Needs a password**. Choose **Create password** under **Sign-in and security** first.

### Remove is greyed out on the password

It says **Turn off two-factor to remove**. Two-factor protects your password, so turn it off first.

### "The account was not deleted. Sign out, sign in again, then retry while the session is fresh."

Without a password, deleting needs a recent sign-in. Sign out, sign back in with your provider, then delete straight away.

### "Praetorium could not load"

The app couldn't reach Praetorium and has nothing saved on the phone yet. Check that the phone can open praetorium.gg in its browser, then return to the app and choose **Try again**.

### "Sign-in did not finish"

The sign-in window closed before handing you back, or its code expired. Return to Praetorium and try the provider again. If it keeps happening, install the latest version of the app, which **Update the app to link GitHub.** also asks for.

## Limits

- Account settings can't change your email address.
- Two-factor only protects password sign-in, and only through an authenticator app.
- Account settings doesn't list your signed-in devices. Changing or resetting your password signs out every other device.
- Deleting an account can't be undone, and nobody can restore it.
