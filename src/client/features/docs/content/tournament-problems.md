## Common problems

### "This event keeps … on" or "This event doesn't allow Legends datasheets…"

The tournament seals matched-play lists unless its organizer allows more under **House rules**. Switch the restriction back on in the picker's **Format restrictions**, or remove the Legends or Crucible unit, then seal again. The tournament page shows what it allows beside its format.

### "Choose a Warlord: this list has none"

Every tournament list names exactly one Character or Epic Hero as its Warlord, even in a team tournament, unless a duel's house rules let lists switch the Warlord off. Make one unit the Warlord in the builder, then seal again.

### "choose a roster built for this event's battle size"

A duel tournament seals lists built at its battle size. A 2v1 or doubles tournament also takes smaller ones, but none built bigger unless the list waives **Points limit** and the house rules allow it. Build or copy a list at a size that fits.

### "choose a roster that spends at most … points"

In a 2v1 or doubles tournament each seat has its own points per army. When allies split their points, the number is what your pair has left after your ally's sealed list. Take units out of the list until it spends no more than that, or ask your ally to seal a smaller one, then seal again.

### "your ally sealed a list saved to … points"

Unless the event lets allies split their points, allies seal lists saved to the same points total. Set your list's points total in the builder to match your ally's, or ask them to change theirs.

### "wait for the organizer to give you a size or team"

In a 2v1 or doubles tournament you can't seal until the organizer sets your size or pairs you with a teammate.

### The format, points or house rules can't be changed

The format, battle size, pairing and points total lock once anyone seals a roster. Points per army, **Allies split their points** and house rules stay open until the reveal, but only to values every sealed roster still fits. If you see "a sealed list would no longer fit", ask that player to seal a roster that fits first. Everything locks for good after the reveal. Start a new tournament to play different rules.

### Pair the next round doesn't appear

The next Swiss round pairs once every table in the current one has finished its battle. If a player has left or won't show, the organizer can drop them from their roster row: their unfinished table then counts as a bye for their opponent.

### Reveal all rosters is greyed out

**Before you can reveal** lists what's left, such as players who haven't sealed, requests to answer, or sizes, teams, Force Dispositions or Warlords still to choose.

## Limits

- Praetorium checks lists against the tournament's format, points and house rules. Anything else your group agreed, such as painting or which books are in, is yours to check.
- Team-wide limits in doubles, across both players' lists, aren't checked.
- Guests can't play in tournaments.
