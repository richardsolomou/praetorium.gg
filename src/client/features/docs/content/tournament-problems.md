## Common problems

### "This event keeps … on" or "This event doesn't allow Legends datasheets…"

The tournament seals matched-play lists unless its organizer allows more under **House rules**. Switch the restriction back on in the picker's **Format restrictions**, or remove the Legends or Crucible unit, then seal again. The tournament page shows what it allows beside its format.

### "Choose a Warlord: this list has none"

Every tournament list needs exactly one Character or Epic Hero as its Warlord, unless a duel's house rules let lists switch the Warlord off. Make one unit the Warlord in the builder, then seal again.

### "choose a roster built for this event's battle size"

A duel seals lists built at its battle size. A 2v1 or doubles tournament also takes smaller ones, but no bigger ones unless the list waives **Points limit** and the house rules allow it. Build or copy a list at a size that fits.

### "choose a roster built to this event's … points"

This tournament plays at its own points total. Build or copy a list to that total.

### "choose a roster that spends at most … points"

In a 2v1 or doubles tournament each seat has its own points per army. When allies split their points, the number is what your pair has left after your ally's sealed list. Take units out until the list fits, or ask your ally to seal a smaller one.

### "your ally sealed a list saved to … points"

Allies seal lists saved to the same points total, unless the tournament lets them split their points. Set your list's points total in the builder to match your ally's, or ask them to change theirs.

### "wait for the organizer to give you a size or team"

In a 2v1 or doubles tournament you can't seal until the organizer sets your size or pairs you with a teammate.

### "the roster deadline has passed"

You can't seal after the deadline. The organizer can move it until the reveal.

### The format, points or house rules can't be changed

The format, battle size, pairing and points total lock once anyone seals a roster. Points per army, **Allies split their points** and house rules stay open until the reveal, but only to values every sealed roster still fits. If you see "a sealed list would no longer fit", ask that player to seal a roster that fits first.

Everything locks for good after the reveal. To play different rules, start a new tournament.

### Pair the next round doesn't appear

The next Swiss round pairs once every table in the current one has finished. If a player has left or won't show, the organizer can drop them from their roster row, and their unfinished table counts as a bye for their opponent.

### Reveal all rosters is greyed out

**Before you can reveal** lists what's left, such as players who haven't sealed, or sizes, teams, Force Dispositions or Warlords still to choose. In doubles it also lists requests to answer.

## Limits

- Praetorium checks lists against the tournament's format, points and house rules. Anything else you've agreed, such as painting or which books are in, is yours to check.
- Team-wide limits in doubles, across both players' lists, aren't checked.
- Guests can't play in tournaments.
