To find a faction, datasheet, detachment, mission or rule from anywhere on the site, choose **Search** at the top of the side menu (the magnifier at the top on a phone), or press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K elsewhere. Once you're signed in, it finds your own rosters and recent battles too.

## What it matches

Datasheets match by name, keyword, ability, weapon, weapon keyword or wargear. When none of those match, search reads what units' abilities do, so `gain 1cp` finds units whose abilities give you a Command point. Each result names what matched.

Detachments match by name, or by their rules, enhancements and stratagems. Rules match by name or by the number they're printed under, so you can type a number you see quoted on a card.

If no datasheet matches, search offers **Close matches** for a misspelt name. The unit picker in the roster builder and each faction's datasheet list match the same way.

## Filter a faction's datasheets

**Matched play**, **Legends** and **Crucible** hide or show datasheets by where they come from. **Keywords** keeps only datasheets that have every keyword you choose, and you can type in it to find a keyword quickly. Once you're signed in, you can also show only your favourites or the datasheets you own.

## Search from your browser's address bar

[Search results](/search) have their own page, so you can add Praetorium to your browser as a custom search engine. In your browser's search engine settings, add one with this URL:

```text
https://praetorium.gg/search?q=%s
```

Give it a shortcut such as `p`. Typing `p` and a unit name in the address bar then opens Praetorium's results.
