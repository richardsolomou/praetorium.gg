To find a faction, datasheet, detachment, mission or rule from anywhere on the site, choose **Search** at the top of the side menu (the magnifier at the top on a phone), or press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K elsewhere. Once you're signed in, it finds your own rosters and recent battles too.

![Search for bolt rifle listing datasheets, each with a line saying what matched, such as Matches Bolt Rifle weapon.](screenshot:search-results 'Each result says what matched')

## What it matches

Datasheets match by name, keyword, ability, weapon, weapon keyword or wargear. When none of those match, search reads what units' abilities do, so `gain 1cp` finds units whose abilities give you a Command point. Each result names what matched. Reading what abilities do needs a connection: offline, search matches everything else.

Detachments match by name, or by their rules, enhancements and stratagems. Rules match by name or by the number they're printed under, so you can type a number you see quoted on a card.

If no datasheet matches, search offers **Close matches** for a misspelt name. Each faction's datasheet list matches the same way, and the unit picker in the roster builder matches everything but what abilities do.

## Read a rule

Most rules show a plain-language summary, written for their rules version by VS Data, an open community project, and checked against the official sources. Linked words open the core rule they name. A weapon or core ability a summary names, such as [LETHAL HITS], shows its own summary when you hover over or tap it, as it does on a datasheet's weapons. Where the official text leaves a point open, a faint note under the summary says so, and the summary reads it one way. [Data sources](/sources) credits where summaries come from.

Summaries are for reading only: the builder, the simulator and battles never work from them. Praetorium never shows a rule in its official wording, so a rule without a summary shows its name and numbers only.

## Filter a faction's datasheets

**Matched play**, **Legends** and **Crucible** hide or show datasheets by where they come from. Legends and Crucible datasheets stay hidden until you turn them on, and what you choose here applies to every faction on this device. **Keywords** keeps only datasheets that have every keyword you choose, and you can type in it to find a keyword quickly. Once you're signed in, you can also show only your favourites or the datasheets you own.

## Search from your browser's address bar

[Search results](/search) have their own page, so you can add Praetorium to your browser as a custom search engine. In your browser's search engine settings, add one with this URL:

```text
https://praetorium.gg/search?q=%s
```

Give it a shortcut such as `p`. Typing `p` and a unit name in the address bar then opens Praetorium's results.
