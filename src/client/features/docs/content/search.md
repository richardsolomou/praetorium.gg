Search finds factions, datasheets, detachments, missions and rules from anywhere on the site. Choose **Search** at the top of the page, or press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K elsewhere. Once you're signed in, it finds your own rosters and recent battles too.

## What it matches

- **Datasheets** by name, keyword, ability, weapon, weapon keyword or wargear. When none of those match, search reads what units' abilities do, so `gain 1cp` finds units whose abilities give you a Command point. Each result names what matched.
- **Detachments** by name, or by their rules, enhancements and stratagems.
- **Rules** by name or by the number they're printed under, so you can type a number you see quoted on a card.

If no datasheet matches, search offers **Close matches** for a misspelt name. The unit picker in the roster builder and each faction's datasheet list match the same way.

A faction's datasheet list can also be filtered. **Matched play**, **Legends** and **Crucible** hide or show datasheets by where they come from, and **Keywords** keeps only datasheets that have every keyword you choose; type in it to find a keyword quickly. Once you're signed in, you can also show only your favourites or the datasheets you own.

## Search from your browser's address bar

[Search results](/search) have their own page, so you can add Praetorium to your browser as a custom search engine. In your browser's search engine settings, add one with this URL:

```text
https://praetorium.gg/search?q=%s
```

Give it a shortcut such as `p`. Typing `p` and a unit name in the address bar then opens Praetorium's results.
