## Common problems

### A weapon shows "Unsupported: …" and that phase has no result

The weapon has a rule the simulator can't calculate. Switch that weapon off to get odds for the rest.

### "See Breakdown" appears instead of a destruction chance

One phase couldn't be calculated, so there's no combined result. The phase that works still shows in **Breakdown**.

### "Choose the attacker's surviving models and weapons to calculate attacks."

In a battle, the battle log doesn't show exactly who survived. Use **Survivors** on that unit.

### "Fix the attacker's attachments to calculate attacks."

The pairing isn't legal, for example a Support model without a squad. Add or remove members until it is.

### Optimize is greyed out

It needs a defender, both units fully loaded, and a legal attacker. It's only on the attacker side.

### "The full search could not finish."

The search hit its time limit. The best loadout found so far is kept.

### "Rules text isn't loaded." under Rules & buffs

The simulator works rules out from their wording, which your browser loads from community sources you choose. Until you choose some, abilities, detachment rules and stratagems are left out. Press **Choose sources** and turn on the ones you want.

### A buff does nothing

Check **Calculation uses**. If a chip is dimmed, hover it for the reason.

### The roster comparison stopped partway

It has a five-minute limit and can be cancelled. Completed pairings are kept. Press **Retry** to finish.

### "Sign in and save both rosters to compare them here."

Roster comparisons use saved lists. Sign in, then save or [import](/docs/import-a-list) the lists.

## Limits

- The simulator doesn't know range, line of sight, positioning or which stratagems you've used. You turn those on.
- Rules it can't calculate are left out or named. It never guesses, so a result with a rule missing isn't the full picture.
- It calculates one attacking unit against one target. The roster comparison doesn't plan targets across your army.
