## Share, export and print

In the library, choose **Share…** in a roster's menu. Under **Who can open it**, pick **Private**, **Unlisted** or **Public**. It changes straight away. You can also change **Access** in **Edit roster setup**.

![The Share dialog with Private, Unlisted and Public under Who can open it, the link with Copy link, and Export text, Exchange code and Print.](screenshot:copy-and-share-dialog 'Who can open it, at the top of the Share dialog')

- **Copy link** (**Share link** on a phone) copies the list's address. On a private list the button reads **Make unlisted and copy**, and makes it Unlisted first.
- **Export text** gives you text to **Copy text** or **Download text**. See [Exchange codes and export](/docs/exchange-and-export) for what it holds.
- **Print** prepares a compact two-column reference pack with a datasheet for each unit, then army and detachment rules, stratagems, and the abilities and weapon rules your units use.

The **Build**/**View** switch in the header hides editing controls for reading at the table.

### After you've played a list

Once other players have seen a private or unlisted list, Praetorium offers to share it with **Share** and the list's name: after a finished battle, once a tournament's rosters are revealed, and after the reveal in your latest hidden-list game or a league's lists. **Make public** shares the saved list as it is now, including any changes since you played or sealed it, and your notes stay private. **No thanks** is remembered for that list, so nothing asks about it again. You can still make it public from **Share…** any time.

## Variants, copies and locking

**Create variant** makes a copy grouped with this list. The variants icon in the header shows **Changes from** the base list and switches between variants. **Make its own roster**, or dragging the variant out of its card in the library, takes it out of the group. If the base leaves, the oldest remaining variant becomes the base.

**Duplicate** makes an independent copy. Both open the new list straight away, and a message confirms which one you're editing. On someone else's list it's **Duplicate to my rosters**, and the copy notes where it came from.

**Lock roster** stops edits until you **Unlock** it. You can still share, export, print and take it to battle.

**Lend to a friend** lets a friend view the list, field it in their battles and seal it for a tournament or hidden-list game, without editing it. Your notes stay yours, and they can **Return it** at any time.

**Archive** hides a list. **Delete** removes it for good. Copies in battles and tournaments are kept either way.

Locking, **Make its own roster** and changing several lists at once all need a connection.

## Folders

**New folder** in the library makes a folder where you are, and a folder can hold folders of its own, up to five deep. Click a folder to open it, or right-click it to rename, move or delete it, and use the path above its lists to go back up. Drag a roster onto a folder to move it there, or choose **Move to folder…** in its menu. Drag a folder onto another to put it inside, or choose **Move folder…** in its menu. A variant always goes with its base list and stays in its folder when it becomes its own roster, and a duplicate stays in the same folder as the list it came from.

Deleting a folder deletes the folders inside it too, and every roster in them moves up to where the folder was; no roster is deleted. A search or filter narrows every folder: each level shows its own matching rosters, and only the folders that hold a match, with how many.

## Change several lists at once

**Select** in the library lets you tick up to 100 lists and **Archive** or **Unarchive**, **Lock** or **Unlock**, **Move to folder**, change **Visibility**, or **Delete** them. On a phone these are under **Actions**. Open **Archived** to tick archived lists.

## Your collection

If you're signed in, **Your collection** below the units compares the list with the models you've counted on [Collection](/collection). It shows models short, to build or to paint, or **Ready to field**, with **Add to wishlist** for what's missing. Models you've marked as able to be another datasheet, such as the other builds of a magnetised tank, can fill it too, and each model is counted once. It never affects points or legality.
