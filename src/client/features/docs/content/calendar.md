Add your league round deadlines and tournament dates to the calendar app you already use. The dates stay up to date: your calendar app checks the link again on its own schedule, from every few hours to once a day.

## Get your calendar link

1. Open [Account settings](/profile).
2. Under **Calendar**, press **Get calendar link**.
3. Press **Copy link**.
4. In your calendar app, add a calendar from a link and paste the link. Google Calendar calls this **From URL**, Apple Calendar **New Calendar Subscription** and Outlook **Subscribe from web**.

## What the calendar shows

- Each round deadline an organizer set in a league you play in.
- The roster deadline and the play day of each tournament you entered or run.

Each date is one moment, such as 19:00 on the deadline day, shown in your calendar's timezone. Hidden-list games don't appear.

A league's dates leave the calendar when you withdraw from the league or leave the group. A tournament's dates leave when you leave it or its organizer drops you.

## Keep the link private

Anyone with your link can see these dates and the names of your leagues and tournaments. The link shows nothing else about you or other players.

If someone else has the link, press **Replace link**. The old link stops working, so add the new link to your calendar app. **Turn off** stops the link and gives you no new one.
