The Praetorium app opens the same site as praetorium.gg, with the same account, rosters and battles. The differences are around the edges: sign-in, offline use, notifications, the screen during a battle and the Apple Watch.

## Sign in on the app

The sign-in page is the same as on the web. Email and password work inside the app. **Continue with Apple**, **Google**, **Discord** or **GitHub** opens a secure sign-in window from your phone, then returns you to the app on its own. GitHub sign-in needs the latest version of the app.

Your account settings are under **Profile** on the **More** tab, and **Sign out** is in the account menu at the top right.

## Use it offline

Once Praetorium has finished loading on your phone, your saved rosters, the reference, the simulator and battles you've opened keep working without a connection. New pages and other players' updates need a connection.

If the app can't reach Praetorium when it opens, it shows the copy saved on your phone. While the phone is offline, a bar at the top reads **Offline · Saved data**.

Changes you make offline are saved on the phone and sync when you're back online. The bar then shows how many changes are waiting; choose **Details** to open **Saved changes**, where **Sync now** tries again and **Export saved changes** downloads them as a file. Keep the app installed until they've synced.

If nothing has been saved on the phone yet, the app shows **Praetorium could not load**. Reconnect and choose **Try again**.

## Notifications

When notifications are available, Home shows **Get notified about your games**. Choose **Allow notifications** for new battles, friend requests and tournament updates, or **Not now** to hide the offer on that phone. Turn them off at any time in your phone's settings. Signing out stops them on that phone.

## During a battle

The screen stays awake while a battle you're seated in is open, and each recorded action gives a light vibration.

## Apple Watch

With an Apple Watch paired to your iPhone, the Praetorium watch app follows the last live battle you opened on the phone, whether you're playing or watching. It shows the round, phase, whose turn it is, the turn timer and each side's VP and CP, and keeps updating while the watch app is open, even with your iPhone locked. When it can't get an update, the status turns orange and reads **Saved** with the time of the last one.

In a battle you're playing, the next pages show your objectives and reminders. **Dismiss on watch** hides a reminder on the watch only. Turn on **Reminder taps** in the watch app's settings for a gentle tap when new reminders arrive while it's open. Signing out on the phone clears the battle from the watch.

## Updates

Most updates reach the app on their own. Some features, such as GitHub sign-in, need a newer version of the app itself. Install the latest version before reporting a sign-in or link problem.
