The Praetorium app opens the same site as praetorium.gg, with the same account, rosters and battles. Sign in with the account you use on the web and everything is already there.

## Get the app

On iPhone and iPad, install [Praetorium from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/praetorium-gg/id6806875244). Safari on those devices also offers it in a banner at the top of praetorium.gg. On a computer, Home shows both store badges under its opening band. Signed in, **Not now** hides them, and they stay away while you have opened the app in the past month.

The Android app is in a closed beta. The **Get it on Google Play** badge on Home shows these steps, with each Google page it opens and the button to tap ringed.

1. Open the [Praetorium Android beta group](https://groups.google.com/g/praetorium-android-beta/about) and choose **Join group**, then **Join group** again on the form that opens. If Google shows only **Sign in**, sign in at the top first.
2. [Opt in to the beta](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/gg.praetorium) and choose **Become a tester**.
3. [Install Praetorium from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=gg.praetorium) with the same Google account. If Google Play says the page isn't found, wait a few minutes or switch Google Play to that account.

Stay in the test for at least 14 days. Google only opens the app to everyone once enough testers have stayed opted in that long.

## Sign in on the app

The sign-in page is the same as on the web. Email and password work inside the app. **Continue with Apple**, **Google**, **Discord** or **GitHub** opens a secure sign-in window from your phone, then returns you to the app on its own. GitHub sign-in needs the latest version of the app.

Account settings open from your name at the top of the **More** tab. **Sign out** is in the account menu at the top right.

## Use it offline

Once Praetorium has finished loading on your phone, your saved rosters, the reference, the simulator and battles you've opened keep working without a connection. New pages and other players' updates need a connection.

If the app can't reach Praetorium when it opens, it shows the copy saved on your phone. While the phone is offline, a bar at the top reads **Offline · Saved data**.

Changes you make offline are saved on the phone and sync when you're back online. The bar then shows how many are waiting. Choose **Details** to open **Saved changes**, where **Sync now** tries again and **Export saved changes** downloads them as a file. Keep the app installed until they've synced.

If nothing has been saved on the phone yet, the app shows **Praetorium could not load**. Reconnect and choose **Try again**.

## Notifications

When notifications are available, Home shows **Get notified about your games**. Choose **Allow notifications** for new battles, friend requests, tournament updates and league deadlines, or **Not now** to hide the offer on that phone.

Tapping a notification opens its page and marks it read. Once you've read a notification anywhere, on the website or another phone, the app clears it from your phone's notification list the next time it updates, and the number on the app's icon counts only the ones you haven't read.

Turn them off in your phone's settings. Signing out stops them on that phone and clears the number on the icon. Every notification also stays under **More** › **Notifications**, where you can read it later; see [Notifications](/docs/notifications).

## During a battle

The screen stays awake while a battle you're seated in is open. The phone also answers your touch, with a tick or tap as you act and a warning when an action is refused or a roster goes over its points limit.

## Apple Watch

With an Apple Watch paired to your iPhone, the Praetorium watch app follows the last live battle you opened on the phone, playing or watching. It shows the round, phase, whose turn it is, the turn timer and each side's VP and CP. It keeps updating while the watch app is open, even with your iPhone locked. When it can't get an update, the status turns orange and reads **Saved** with the time of the last one.

In a battle you're playing, the next pages show your objectives and reminders. **Dismiss on watch** hides a reminder on the watch only. Turn on **Reminder taps** in the watch app's settings for a gentle tap when new reminders arrive while it's open.

Signing out on the phone clears the battle from the watch.

## Updates

Most updates reach the app on their own. Some features, such as GitHub sign-in, need a newer version of the app itself. Install the latest version before reporting a sign-in or link problem.
