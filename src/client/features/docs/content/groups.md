A group keeps a running leaderboard of the games you play together. There's no end date, so play each other whenever you like.

To start one, press **New group** on [Groups](/groups), then use **Copy invite link** on the group page to bring people in. **Group guide**, the question mark in the page's top corner, walks you through it.

## Set up a group

Under **Who can find it**, **Public** lists the group on the groups page. **Private link** keeps it to people you send the link to, and only members can see its leaderboard.

Under **Who gets in**, choose **Anyone who joins** or **You approve**. Requests wait at the top of the group page under **Asking to join**. Switching to **Anyone who joins** lets in everyone waiting.

The owner can pick admins from the members in **Edit group**, in the group page's ⋯ menu. Admins can edit the group and manage members. Only the owner can remove an admin or delete the group.

## Play for the leaderboard

1. On the group page, press **New game**.
2. Pick a duel, solo vs pair or doubles, and who plays.
3. Press **Start battle**.

You can also pick the group under **Group** when you set up a battle anywhere else. It only shows when everyone in the battle is a member, and you can change it until the battle starts. Games with a guest or practice opponent can't count, and neither can manually tracked games.

Ratings work as on the site-wide [leaderboard](/leaderboard): everyone starts at 1000, beating a higher-rated player gains more, a concession is a loss, and allies share their side's result, rated on their average. Group games still count on the site-wide leaderboard too.

## The group page

**Up next for you**, beside the page on a computer and at its top on a phone, shows the one thing waiting on you: a battle you're playing, a list to seal, revealed lists to play, or your next league game.

The **Overview** tab has the current hidden-list game, your unfinished battles, **Recent games** and **Past hidden-list games**. **Leaderboard** rates every game played for the group. **League** has the round robin, with its own table and fixtures.

## Hidden-list games

Everyone seals a list in secret, then you reveal them together. An organizer sets one up:

1. Press **New game** and turn on **Hide lists until everyone has sealed**.
2. Press **Set up game** and pick a format: a duel, solo vs pair, or doubles. Points and house rules work as they do in a tournament (see [Run a tournament](/docs/run-a-tournament)).
3. Everyone seals their list from **Up next** on **Overview**.

In a duel, any member seals a list. After the reveal, use **Play** beside the person you're playing that evening. Nobody has to play every participant.

In solo vs pair and doubles, pick your side with **Join**, then seal a list for it. Each pair chooses its Force Disposition and whose Warlord leads before the reveal. Afterwards, **Start battle** sets up the one battle between the sides. Results go on the group's leaderboard.

### Before the reveal

An organizer can change the game from **Edit** in its ⋯ menu. Once anyone seals a list, the format and battle size lock. Points per army, **Allies split their points** and house rules can still change as long as every sealed list still fits them.

**Remind players** notifies each player in the game who hasn't sealed yet, once a day.

### After the reveal

Each list shows its faction and points. Use **Simulate** beside another player's list to compare your sealed armies. In a team game, **Simulate** sits on the other side's heading and sets your side's lists against theirs.

### When a game ends

Once a one-on-one game's lists are revealed, an organizer can set or roll its battlefield, so both players can prepare. The players can't change it, and the organizer can until the battle starts.

Starting a new hidden-list game moves the previous one to **Past hidden-list games**. A scheduled game also moves there after its scheduled day ends, and an undated game after the day its lists were revealed. Days end at midnight in the timezone of the organizer who created it. An undated game with unrevealed lists stays open until replaced.

A game with a battle still being played stays under **Up next** until that battle ends. Past games keep their results, and you can still resume battles already underway. View or simulate revealed armies under **Lists**. You can't start another battle or change lists in a past game.

## Leagues

A league is a round robin: everyone who joins plays every other player once, one-on-one. A group runs one league at a time.

1. An organizer presses **Start a league** on the **League** tab.
2. Members press **Join league** while it's a draft.
3. With at least 3 players in, an organizer presses **Publish**. After that nobody else can join, and the fixtures can't be drawn again.
4. Press **Start battle** on one of your fixtures. Each of you picks a list at setup. You can play rounds in any order.

Only organizers see the draft fixtures. **Draw again** shuffles them, and the same draw stays until someone joins, leaves or it's drawn again. With an odd number of players, each player has one bye. Fixture games also count on the group's leaderboard.

An organizer can set a deadline for each round. A game not finished by then shows as **Overdue**. Nothing is scored for it.

On the table, a win or a bye is worth 3 points and a draw 1. Ties are broken by victory points, then by the points of everyone you've played. Every bye counts from the start.

To leave, choose **Withdraw from league** in the **Fixtures** ⋯ menu. Your unplayed fixtures become void, and your finished games stay on the table. Leaving the group withdraws you too.

**End league**, in the same menu, removes the fixtures and table. Their battles stay.

## Leave or delete a group

**Leave group** takes you off the leaderboard. Your games still count for your opponents, and your results come back if you rejoin. The owner can't leave their own group.

**Delete group** removes the group, its members, its leaderboard, its league and its hidden-list games. Its battles stay.
