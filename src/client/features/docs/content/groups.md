A group keeps a running leaderboard of the games you play together. There's no end date, so play each other whenever you like.

To start one, press **New group** on [Groups](/groups), then use **Copy invite link** on the group page to bring people in. **Group guide**, the question mark in the page's top corner, walks you through it.

![A group page header with the group's name, members and organizers, and New game and Copy invite link beside it.](screenshot:groups-page 'Copy invite link, beside New game')

## Set up a group

Under **Who can find it**, **Public** lists the group on the groups page. **Private link** keeps it to people you send the link to, and only members can see its leaderboard.

Under **Who gets in**, choose **Anyone who joins** or **You approve**. Requests wait at the top of the group page under **Asking to join**. Switching to **Anyone who joins** lets in everyone waiting.

The owner can pick admins from the members in **Edit group**, in the group page's ⋯ menu. Admins can edit the group and manage members. Only the owner can remove an admin or delete the group.

## Play for the leaderboard

1. On the group page, press **New game**.
2. Pick a duel, solo vs pair or doubles, and who plays.
3. Press **Start battle**.

![The New game dialog with Hide lists until everyone has sealed, Duel, Solo vs pair and Doubles, an opponent, and Start battle.](screenshot:groups-new-game 'Hide lists until everyone has sealed turns a game into a hidden-list game')

You can also pick the group under **Record for a group** in a battle's **Tracking** step. It only shows when everyone in the battle is a member, and you can change it until the battle starts. Games with a guest or where you play both sides can't count, and neither can manually tracked games.

Ratings work as on the site-wide [leaderboard](/leaderboard): everyone starts at 1000, beating a higher-rated player gains more, a concession is a loss, and allies share their side's result, rated on their average. Group games still count on the site-wide leaderboard too.

## The group page

**Up next for you**, beside the page on a computer and at its top on a phone, shows the one thing waiting on you: a battle you're playing, a list to seal, revealed lists to play, or your next league game. It's hidden when nothing is waiting.

The **Overview** tab sums the group up: how many games it has played, who has played lately and which battles are in play this week, its most played armies, the top of the leaderboard with each player's latest results, the league's progress and table, **Recent results**, **Head to head** records between members, and the current and past hidden-list games. **Battles** lists every game played for the group, with [filters](/docs/filter-lists). **Leaderboard** rates every game played for the group. **League** has the round robin, with its own table and fixtures.

## Hidden-list games

Everyone seals a list in secret, then you reveal them together. An organizer sets one up:

1. Press **New game** and turn on **Hide lists until everyone has sealed**.
2. Press **Set up game** and pick a format: a duel, solo vs pair, or doubles. Points and house rules work as they do in a tournament (see [Run a tournament](/docs/run-a-tournament)).
3. Everyone seals their list from **Up next** on **Overview**.

To have every battle play to the same chess clock, choose each side's time under **Chess clock** in the game's settings.

In a duel, any member seals a list. After the reveal, use **Play** beside the person you're playing that evening. Nobody has to play every participant.

In solo vs pair and doubles, pick your side with **Join**, then seal a list for it. A solo vs pair game plays 2,000 against 2,000, with the pair spending its 2,000 between them however they agree. Each pair chooses its Force Disposition and whose Warlord leads before the reveal, and a solo player chooses theirs when sealing. Afterwards, **Start battle** sets up the one battle between the sides. Results go on the group's leaderboard.

### Before the reveal

An organizer can change the game from **Edit** in its ⋯ menu. Once anyone seals a list, the format and battle size lock. The solo player's points, doubles points per army, **Allies split their points** and house rules can still change as long as every sealed list still fits them.

**Remind players** notifies each player in the game who hasn't sealed yet, once a day.

### After the reveal

Each list shows its faction and points. Use **Simulate** beside another player's list to compare your sealed armies. In a team game, **Simulate** sits on the other side's heading and sets your side's lists against theirs.

When a game has exactly two sides, an organizer can press **Set battlefield** after the reveal to pick or **Roll** its battlefield, so everyone can prepare before anyone starts the battle. That covers a duel between two players, solo vs pair and doubles, and each side's Force Disposition decides the layouts on offer. The players are notified and can't change it, and the organizer can until the battle starts. A duel with more than two players has no single table to set, so those players choose at the table.

### When a game ends

Starting a new hidden-list game moves the previous one to **Past hidden-list games**. A scheduled game also moves there after its scheduled day ends, and an undated game after the day its lists were revealed. Days end at midnight in the timezone of the organizer who created it. An undated game with unrevealed lists stays open until replaced.

A game with a battle still being played stays under **Up next** until that battle ends. Past games keep their results, and you can still resume battles already underway. View or simulate revealed armies under **Lists**. You can't start another battle or change lists in a past game.

## Leagues

A league is a round robin: everyone who joins plays every other player once, one-on-one. A group runs one league at a time.

1. An organizer presses **Start a league** on the **League** tab.
2. Members press **Join league** on the **League** tab while it's a draft. **Up next for you** links there too.
3. With at least 3 players in, an organizer presses **Publish**. After that nobody else can join, and the fixtures can't be drawn again.
4. Press **Start battle** on one of your fixtures. Each of you picks a list at setup. You can play rounds in any order.

![A league round with a finished game and its score, your fixture with Start battle, and a bye.](screenshot:groups-league 'Start battle, on your fixture')

Only organizers see the draft fixtures. **Draw again** shuffles them, and the same draw stays until someone joins, leaves or it's drawn again. With an odd number of players, each player has one bye, which scores nothing. Tracked fixture games also count on the group's leaderboard.

An organizer can press **Set deadline** beside each round once the league is published. A game not finished by then shows as **Overdue**. Nothing is scored for it, and it stays open to play late. A league game counts when its battle is tracked here and finished, or when an organizer presses **Enter score** on it and types both scores for a game that wasn't tracked here. An entered score counts for the league table but not the leaderboard. About a day before a deadline, both players of each game in that round that isn't finished get a notification. A moved deadline sends a new one. To see the deadlines in your own calendar app, see [Add dates to your calendar](/docs/calendar).

An organizer can also press **Set chess clock** to give each side the same time in every fixture. Fixtures that haven't started play to it, and their players can't change it.

On the table, a win or a bye is worth 3 points and a draw 1. Ties are broken by victory points, then by the points of everyone you've played. Every bye counts from the start.

To leave, choose **Withdraw from league** in the **Fixtures** ⋯ menu. Your unplayed fixtures become void, and your finished games stay on the table. Leaving the group withdraws you too. If a player stops turning up, an organizer can remove them from the group under **Members**, which withdraws them the same way.

Once every fixture is played, void or a bye, the league is complete: the **League** tab, the **Overview** and **Up next for you** name the winner, and the table is final. Players level on points and both tiebreaks share first place. A complete league stays until an organizer ends it.

To run it again, an organizer presses **Start the next league** on the complete league. It ends the league and opens a draft for the next one, with every player who didn't withdraw and is still in the group already in, and the same chess clock. Anyone else in the group can join, or a player can leave, before an organizer publishes it. When the league sealed lists, the next one seals to the same points and house rules, and everyone seals a new list to join it.

**End league**, in the same menu, closes it at any time without starting another. An ended league's final table stays under **Past leagues** on the **League** tab, with its winner, and its battles stay.

### Seal lists before a league starts

To have everyone commit to one army for the whole league, turn on **Seal lists before it starts** when you press **Start a league**, then choose the points and house rules. Members join by pressing **Seal a list to join** and picking a saved list, and can swap it with **Swap your list** until the league is published. A list that offers more than one Force Disposition and names none asks which one it plays for the whole league as you seal it. Nobody sees anyone else's list until then.

**Publish** reveals every list at once, and every fixture is played with them: **Start battle** sets up the game with both sealed lists already in place. The revealed lists stay under **Lists** on the **League** tab.

## Leave or delete a group

**Leave group** takes you off the leaderboard. Your games still count for your opponents, and your results come back if you rejoin. The owner can't leave their own group.

**Delete group** removes the group, its members, its leaderboard, its league and its hidden-list games. Its battles stay.
