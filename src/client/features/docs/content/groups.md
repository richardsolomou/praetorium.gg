A group keeps a running leaderboard of the games you play together. There's no end date: play each other whenever you like, and every game you play for the group goes on its leaderboard. Start one with **New group** on [Groups](/groups).

## The group page

**Up next for you**, beside the page on a computer and at its top on a phone, shows the one thing waiting on you: a battle you're playing, a list to seal, revealed lists to play, or your next league game.

The page has three tabs:

- **Overview**: the current hidden-list game, your unfinished battles, the group's recent games, and past hidden-list games. Each battle says whether it was a hidden-list, league or friendly game.
- **Leaderboard**: every game played for the group, rated.
- **League**: the round robin, with its own table and fixtures.

## Set up a group

- **Who can find it**: **Public** lists the group on the groups page. **Private link** keeps it to people you send the link to.
- **Who gets in**: **Anyone who joins**, or **You approve** each request. Requests wait at the top of the group page under **Asking to join**. Switching to **Anyone who joins** lets in everyone waiting.
- **Admins**: the owner picks them from the members in **Edit group**, in the group page's ⋯ menu. The members list marks the owner and admins. Admins can edit the group and manage members. Only the owner can remove an admin or delete the group.

Use **Copy invite link** on the group page to bring people in. Only members can see a private group's leaderboard.

## Play for the leaderboard

On the group page, press **New game**, pick a duel, solo vs pair or doubles and who plays, and press **Start battle**. Or, when you set up a battle anywhere else, pick the group under **Group**. You'll only see it when everyone in the battle is a member. Games with a guest or practice opponent, and manually tracked games, can't count. You can change the choice until the battle starts.

When the game ends, the result goes on the group's leaderboard. Ratings work as on the site-wide [leaderboard](/leaderboard): everyone starts at 1000, beating a higher-rated player gains more, a concession is a loss, and allies share their side's result, rated on their average. Group games still count on the site-wide leaderboard too.

## Hidden-list games

An organizer can press **New game**, turn on **Hide lists until everyone has sealed**, press **Set up game**, and pick its format: a duel, solo vs pair, or doubles. It shows under **Up next** on **Overview**, where everyone seals a list in secret and you reveal them together.

- **Duel**: any member seals a list. After reveal, use **Play** beside the person you’re playing that evening; nobody has to play every participant.
- **Solo vs pair** and **doubles**: pick your side with **Join**, then seal a list for it. Each pair chooses its Force Disposition and whose Warlord leads before the reveal. Afterwards, **Start battle** sets up the one battle between the sides.

Each hidden-list game has its own format, points and house rules, set as a tournament's are (see [Run a tournament](/docs/run-a-tournament)). Before the reveal an organizer can change them from **Edit** in the game's ⋯ menu. Once anyone seals a list, the format and battle size lock, but points per army, **Allies split their points** and house rules can still change as long as every sealed list still fits them.

Before the reveal, an organizer can press **Remind players** to send a notification to each player in the game who hasn't sealed a list yet, once a day.

After reveal, use **Simulate** beside another player’s list to compare your sealed armies. In a team game, **Simulate** sits on the other side, or on **Lists** once the game is underway, and sets your side's lists against theirs.

The results go on the group's leaderboard. Starting a new hidden-list game automatically moves the previous one to **Past hidden-list games**, on **Overview**. A scheduled game also moves there after its scheduled day ends; an undated game moves after the day its lists were revealed. Days end at midnight in the timezone of the organizer who created the game. An undated game with unrevealed lists stays open until replaced. A game with a battle still being played stays under **Up next** until that battle ends, though nobody can start another battle from it.

Past games keep their results and any battles already underway, which you can still resume. View or simulate revealed armies under **Lists**. You can’t start another battle or change lists in a past game. **Recent games**, on **Overview**, shows the group's latest battles of every kind.

## Leagues

A league is a round robin: everyone who joins plays every other player once, one-on-one. A group runs one league at a time.

- **Start**: an organizer presses **Start a league** on the **League** tab. Members press **Join league** while it's a draft. Only organizers see the draft fixtures. **Draw again** shuffles them, and the same draw stays until someone joins, leaves or it's drawn again.
- **Publish**: needs at least 3 players. After that nobody else can join, and the fixtures can't be drawn again. With an odd number of players, each player has one bye.
- **Play**: press **Start battle** on one of your fixtures. Each of you picks a list at setup. You can play rounds in any order. Fixture games also count on the group's leaderboard.
- **Deadlines**: an organizer can set a deadline for each round. A game not finished by then shows as **Overdue**. Nothing is scored for it.
- **Table**: a win or a bye is worth 3 points and a draw 1. Ties are broken by victory points, then by the points of everyone you've played. Every bye counts from the start.
- **Withdraw**: choose **Withdraw from league** in the **Fixtures** ⋯ menu. Your unplayed fixtures become void, and your finished games stay on the table. Leaving the group withdraws you too.

**End league**, in the same menu, removes the fixtures and table. Their battles stay.

## Leave or delete a group

**Leave group** takes you off the leaderboard. Your games still count for your opponents, and your results come back if you rejoin. The owner can't leave their own group. **Delete group** removes the group, its members, its leaderboard, its league and its hidden-list games. Its battles stay.
