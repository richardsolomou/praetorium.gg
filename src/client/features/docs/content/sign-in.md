You can build one list, browse the reference and run the simulator without an account. You need one to save rosters, start battles, join tournaments and add friends. Accounts are free and for players aged 13 and over.

## Create an account

1. Open the account menu and choose **Sign in**. On a computer it's at the bottom of the side menu. On a phone it's the picture button at the top right.
2. On the sign-in page, choose **I need an account**.
3. Enter **Your name**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 10 characters.
4. Choose **Create account**. You're signed in straight away.

To skip the password, choose **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Discord** or **Continue with GitHub** instead.

Your name is what other players see in battles, rosters and friend lists. You can change it later under **Display name** in [Account settings](/profile). If you hide your name when signing up with Apple, Praetorium asks you to choose one. Your email stays private.

When you sign up with a password, Praetorium sends you a verification email. You can play without verifying, but see [Verify your email](/docs/account-security#verify-your-email) for why it's worth doing.

## Sign in

Enter your **Email** and **Password** and choose **Sign in**, or use the same Apple, Google, Discord or GitHub account you signed up or linked with. If you've set up two-factor authentication, the page then asks for an **Authenticator code**. See [Two-factor authentication](/docs/account-security#two-factor-authentication).

You stay signed in on that device until you sign out, or until you haven't used Praetorium there for 90 days.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. Type your email into **Email**, then choose **Forgot password?**. Without an email the page says **Enter your email first.**
2. The page says **If that account exists, a reset link has been sent.** Open the link in the email within an hour.
3. Enter a **New password** and choose **Reset password**, then sign in with it.

Resetting your password signs you out on every other device.

## Sign out

Open the account menu and choose **Sign out**. If offline changes haven't synced yet, Praetorium opens **Saved changes** instead and asks you to sync, export or discard them first.
