Open the invite link your organizer sent, or find a public tournament on [Tournaments](/tournaments), and choose **Join tournament**. If the organizer approves each player, you wait until they let you in.

## Seal a roster

1. Open **Choose a roster**. It lists your rosters built to the tournament's points, and to its points total when it sets one, plus any that fit under **Lent by friends**.
2. Pick one and press **Seal**.

Nobody sees your roster until the reveal, not even the organizer, and you can **Swap roster** until then. Editing the saved roster afterwards doesn't change the sealed copy. The tournament page offers a replacement when the saved one has changed.

If the tournament has a roster deadline, seal before it. After it you can't seal, and you sit the tournament out.

The tournament page shows what it allows beside its format, such as **Matched play** or **Legends allowed · Restrictions off: Warlord**. A roster that breaks those rules can't be sealed.

In a 2v1 or doubles tournament the organizer gives you your size and teammate first. Your team then chooses the Force Disposition it plays and whose Warlord leads, before the reveal.

If a seal is refused, look up the message in [Tournament problems](/docs/tournament-problems).

## After the reveal

Every roster opens at once. Each player's row has **View roster** and **Simulate** for their list. In a doubles or 2v1 tournament, **Simulate** sits on the other side's heading.

Start a tournament battle from the tournament page against another entrant, and both sealed rosters are added for you. If you and an opponent are both in a tournament, starting a battle anywhere offers to play it in the tournament.

Finished battles fill the results table, which counts the tournament's latest 500 battles.

Once the rosters are out you can also make the original saved roster public with **Share your sealed roster?**, or decline.

## Swiss rounds

In a tournament with Swiss rounds you don't choose your opponent. When the organizer pairs a round, your table shows on the tournament page with **Start battle**. With notifications on in the app, you hear when each round is paired. Once your battle has started, **Resume battle** on the tournament page takes you back to it.

A bye counts as a win. The next round is paired once every table has finished, and earlier results are then locked. If a result is wrong, ask the organizer to reopen it, then correct the score and finish the battle again.

To leave, press the withdraw button on your roster row, then **Withdraw**. You aren't paired again and can't rejoin, and your results stay in the table. If your table in the current round hasn't finished, your opponent takes it as a bye. If your opponent withdraws or the organizer drops them before your table finishes, you get the bye.
