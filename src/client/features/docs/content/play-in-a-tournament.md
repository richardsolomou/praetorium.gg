Open the invite link your organizer sent, or find a public tournament on [Tournaments](/tournaments), and choose **Join tournament**. If the organizer approves each player, you wait until they let you in.

## Seal a roster

**Choose a roster** lists your rosters built to the tournament's points, and to its points total when it sets one. Pick one and press **Seal**. Nobody sees it until the reveal, the organizer included, and you can **Swap roster** until then. Editing the saved roster afterwards doesn't change the sealed copy, and the tournament page offers a replacement when the saved one has changed. If the tournament has a roster deadline, seal before it: after it you can't seal, and you sit the tournament out.

The tournament page shows what it allows beside its format, such as **Matched play** or **Legends allowed · Restrictions off: Warlord**. A roster that breaks a restriction the tournament keeps, or brings Legends or Crucible datasheets it doesn't allow, can't be sealed. Tournaments created before organizers could choose this accept whatever a list switches off except the Warlord, after asking you to confirm.

In a 2v1 or doubles tournament the organizer gives you your size and teammate first. Your team then chooses the Force Disposition it plays and whose Warlord leads, before the reveal.

## After the reveal

Every roster opens at once. Each player's card has **View roster** and **Simulate** for their list. Start a tournament battle from the tournament page against another entrant, and both sealed rosters are added for you. If you and an opponent are both in a tournament, starting a battle anywhere offers to play it in the tournament. Finished battles fill the results table, which counts the tournament's latest 500 battles.

In a tournament with Swiss rounds you don't choose your opponent: when the organizer pairs a round, your table shows on the tournament page with **Start battle**. With notifications on in the app, you hear when each round is paired. A bye counts as a win. The next round is paired once every table has finished.

Once the rosters are out you can also make the original saved roster public with **Share your sealed roster?**, or decline.
