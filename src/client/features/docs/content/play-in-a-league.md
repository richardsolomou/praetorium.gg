Open the invite link your organizer sent, or find a public league on [Leagues](/leagues), and choose **Join league**. If the organizer approves each player, you wait until they let you in.

## Seal a roster

**Choose a roster** lists your rosters built to the event's points. Pick one and press **Seal**. Nobody sees it until the reveal, the organizer included, and you can **Swap roster** until then. Editing the saved roster afterwards doesn't change the sealed copy, and the event page offers a replacement when the saved one has changed.

The league page shows what the event allows beside its format, such as **Matched play** or **Legends allowed · Restrictions off: Warlord**. A roster that breaks a restriction the event keeps, or brings Legends or Crucible datasheets it doesn't allow, can't be sealed. Events created before organizers could choose this accept whatever a list switches off except the Warlord, after asking you to confirm.

In a 2v1 or doubles event the organizer gives you your size and teammate first. Your team then chooses the Force Disposition it plays and whose Warlord leads, before the reveal.

## After the reveal

Every roster opens at once. Each player's card has **View roster** and **Simulate against** for their list. Start a league battle from the event page against someone in the event, and both sealed rosters are added for you. If you and an opponent are both in an event, starting a battle anywhere offers to play it in the league. Finished battles fill the results table, which counts the event's latest 500 battles.

Once the rosters are out you can also make the original saved roster public with **Share your league roster?**, or decline.
