## Common problems

### Start battle is greyed out

The bar beside it lists what's left: an army, the battle size, an allied side's Warlord, a Force Disposition, the battlefield, defender, mission cards or first turn.

### "Choose rosters with the same points budget for each side."

Your lists were built for different sizes or budgets. Pick lists built for the same points, or edit one.

### "an army spends more than its … points"

In a 2v1 or doubles battle each side has its own points per army, shown beside **Battle size**. Change it with **Set points per army**, or bring a list that spends less.

### "choose lists saved to the same points total"

Allies on one side bring lists saved to the same points total. Change one ally's list, or set its points total in the builder to match.

### The roster chooser says none of your saved rosters is built for the battle's points

The battle size is already set by the other armies. Choose **Choose a different battle size** or build a list for it in [Rosters](/rosters).

### "This list waives a rule."

The roster skips a rule of its battle size. You can **Bring it anyway**, and everyone at the table sees what it waives.

### The phase button is blocked

The message under it tells you why, usually unfinished scoring from the previous turn, or secondary missions that still need drawing or reviewing.

### "Check your connection and try again."

The battle action could not reach the server. Check your connection, then try the action again. If drawing secondary missions failed, choose **Draw at random** again.

### The guest link says someone already joined

Another device took the seat. Take the seat back and send the new link.

### "This battle is not open."

Nobody is seated at the battle any more, so there is nothing to watch.

### Your BattleBase tagline doesn't show the code yet

Save the tagline on BattleBase, check it shows the whole code, and choose **Import battles** again. The code belongs to your Praetorium account and that BattleBase username, so a code shown to someone else won't work for you.

### Some games were left out

Only finished, public games you played in can be imported. Make a game public in the app, then import again.

### An army shows as a list rather than units

Praetorium rebuilds an army only when it finds every unit in it. Where one isn't in its datasheets, the army shows as the app listed it, with nothing left out.

## Limits

- Praetorium records what you tell it. It doesn't roll dice, check movement, range or line of sight, or judge whether a score was earned. In a manual battle it doesn't check scores or CP at all.
- There's no computer opponent. A practice opponent is a side you play yourself.
- Guests can't sit on your side or play in tournaments.
- A finished battle can't be reopened from the app, except by a Swiss tournament's organizer from the tournament page.
