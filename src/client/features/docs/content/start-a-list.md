You can build a list without an account. This device then keeps one roster in this browser; the footer's **Save roster** takes you through signing up and saves that list to your account. Variants, saved loadouts, sharing, locking, lending and battles need an account.

Open [Rosters](/rosters) and choose **New roster**. The setup dialog asks for:

- **Faction**, and **Rules version** when your faction has more than one.
- **Battle size**. **King of the Colosseum · 600 points**, **Incursion · 1,000 points**, **Strike Force · 2,000 points** or **Onslaught · 3,000 points**. The box beside the size takes your own points limit from 500 to 10,000; leave it empty for the size's full points. It changes only the points ceiling, and the named size still sets every other limit. A tournament can set its own points total for every roster, and King of the Colosseum has a fixed total.
- **Detachments**. Pick up to three. The counter shows **DP used** against the size's allowance: 2 DP at Incursion, 3 DP at Strike Force, no limit at Onslaught. The allowance only applies once you take more than one detachment. **Find a detachment** filters by name or Force disposition.
- **Force disposition**. If your detachments offer only one, it's chosen for you. If they offer several, pick one; it decides your primary mission in battle.
- **Roster name**. Leave it empty and the list is named from your army.
- **Access**. **Private**, **Unlisted** (anyone with the link) or **Public** (listed on your profile and for anyone to find). A visitor's list is always private.

Choose **Create roster**. While something is missing, the dialog says what, such as **Choose a detachment.** or **Choose a Force disposition.**

You can also start from a faction or detachment page with **Start a roster**, or from a datasheet's **Add to roster**.
