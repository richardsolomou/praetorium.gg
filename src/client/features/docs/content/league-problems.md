## Common problems

### "This event keeps … on" or "This event doesn't allow Legends datasheets…"

The event seals matched-play lists unless its organizer allows more under **House rules**. Switch the restriction back on in the picker's **Format restrictions**, or remove the Legends or Crucible unit, then seal again. The league page shows what the event allows beside its format.

### "Choose a Warlord: this list has none"

Every league list names exactly one Character or Epic Hero as its Warlord, even in a team event, unless a duel's house rules let lists switch the Warlord off. Make one unit the Warlord in the builder, then seal again.

### "choose a roster built for your assigned size"

The roster was built to a different number of points than the event or your assigned size. Build or copy a list at that size. A custom points budget isn't accepted either: remove it first.

### "wait for the organizer to give you a size or team"

In a 2v1 or doubles event you can't seal until the organizer sets your size or pairs you with a teammate.

### The format, points or house rules can't be changed

They lock once anyone seals a roster, and for good after the reveal. Start the next event to play different rules.

### Reveal all rosters is greyed out

**Before you can reveal** lists what's left, such as players who haven't sealed, requests to answer, or sizes, teams, Force Dispositions or Warlords still to choose.

## Limits

- Praetorium checks lists against the event's format, points and house rules. Anything else your group agreed, such as painting or which books are in, is yours to check.
- Team-wide limits in doubles, across both players' lists, aren't checked.
- Guests can't play in leagues.
